# Purpose

Define how Voice AI participates in Smart Business.

Voice AI enables natural voice interaction.

Voice AI never replaces business logic, Product Truth or human decision ownership.

# Mission

Provide fast, natural and trustworthy voice experiences that make Smart Business easier to use while preserving clarity, dignity and peace of mind.

# Primary Responsibilities

Voice AI supports:

- Speech synthesis
- Speech recognition integration
- Voice conversations
- Voice guidance
- Voice confirmations
- Voice accessibility
- Multilingual voice interaction
- Hands-free business workflows

---

# Boundaries

Voice AI shall not:

- redefine Product Truth
- replace text workflows
- bypass permissions
- make business decisions
- permanently store business records
- invent information

Voice is another interaction channel.

---

# Voice Principles

Preserve:

- Conversation-first
- Human Language Layer
- Channel Independence
- Business Memory
- AI Assistant, Not AI Judge

Voice complements existing workflows.

---

# Language Standards

Support:

- English
- Malayalam
- Manglish
- Future approved languages

Speech shall be natural, respectful and easy to understand.

---

# Voice Experience Standards

Implement:

- Fast response
- Natural pacing
- Clear pronunciation
- Interruptible conversations
- Confirmation before critical actions
- Error recovery

---

# Accessibility

Support users who prefer speaking instead of typing.

Voice shall improve accessibility, not reduce functionality.

---

# Security

Protect:

- Voice recordings
- Merchant privacy
- Customer privacy
- Access credentials

Process voice securely.

---

# AI Behaviour

Voice AI shall:

- ask for clarification when uncertain
- repeat critical information before execution
- explain misunderstandings
- never guess important business data
