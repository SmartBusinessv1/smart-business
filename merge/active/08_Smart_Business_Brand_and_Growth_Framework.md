# 11. Language Accessibility Principle

Smart Business should never make a merchant feel less intelligent
because of language choice.

English, Malayalam, and Manglish users receive equal respect.

Language is only a bridge.

Business wisdom belongs to the owner.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Public Website Language Strategy

Public pages must support:

English

Malayalam

Manglish

Applicable pages:

-   Home page (/)
-   How It Works (/how-it-works)
-   Start onboarding (/start)

Users should be able to choose their preferred language.

Do not depend on direct machine translation.

Each language version should preserve emotion and cultural meaning.

Example:

English:

Your AI Business Manager on WhatsApp.

Manglish:

Ningalude business nokkan oru smart WhatsApp sahayi.

The goal is understanding.

Not literal translation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Language Memory

When a user selects a language:

Remember preference.

Apply consistently across:

-   website
-   onboarding quiz
-   WhatsApp replies
-   dashboard experience

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Legal Transparency Language Rule

Official legal documents:

-   Privacy Policy
-   Terms of Service
-   Subscription Terms

will maintain English as the primary legal version.

To improve transparency:

Provide Malayalam explanation/download option.

Purpose:

Help every merchant understand what they agree to.

The Malayalam version exists for clarity and accessibility.

Final legal wording must be reviewed with a qualified professional.
