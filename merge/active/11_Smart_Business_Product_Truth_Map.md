# Source Authority

This document is the official Smart Business Product Truth authority.

Purpose:

Define:
- What Smart Business contains
- Why each feature exists

- Expected user experience
- User authority boundaries
- Product promise limits

Source 12 — Product Execution and Release Framework must build from this truth and must not guess feature behaviour.

---

# Lighthouse Product Principle

Smart Business exists to improve clarity, capability, freedom, and peace of mind for business owners.

Principles:

- Technology serves humans.
- AI assists, humans decide.
- Business owners own their data.
- Systems should reduce complexity, not increase it.
- Features exist to create practical value.

---

# Smart Business User Authority Model

## Owner

Highest authority.

Can:
- access Ask CFO
- view business analytics
- approve changes
- manage users
- control subscriptions
- export business data

---

## Manager (Business Role)

Delegated authority.

Permissions decided by owner.

Can assist operations but does not automatically receive owner intelligence access.

---

## Employee

Operational user.

Can:
- add permitted transactions
- upload permitted receipts/documents
- scan attendance QR
- view own attendance
- request corrections

Cannot:
- access Ask CFO
- see profit intelligence
- see full analytics
- edit own attendance directly
- access owner reports

---

## Supplier

Communication participant.

Can:
- receive purchase/reorder communication
- confirm through text

Cannot:
- access dashboard
- view sales
- view analytics
- access business intelligence

---

## Customer

Business participant.

Can:
- place orders
- receive order updates
- choose preferred language
- view own order status
- receive invoices or receipts where applicable

Cannot:
- access dashboard
- access Ask CFO
- view business analytics
- access other customer information
- access business intelligence

# Conversation First

Smart Business is conversation-first, not platform-first.

Conversation is the enduring product experience.

Current approved conversation channels include:

- WhatsApp
- Smart Business Conversation Workspace

Future approved channels may be added without changing the product identity.

### Smart Business Conversation Workspace

Purpose:

Provide a native Smart Business conversation experience inside the dashboard.

Supports:

- text
- voice
- images
- documents
- Ask CFO
- reminders
- AI conversations

The Conversation Workspace shares:

- the same business memory
- the same AI intelligence
- the same permissions
- the same business identity

It is not a separate system.

It is another way to converse with Smart Business.

### Channel Independence

Business continuity shall never depend upon a single communication channel.

All approved channels shall share:

- business memory
- permissions
- AI understanding
- conversation history
- business identity

### Dual Authentication

Smart Business supports multiple approved authentication methods including:

- WhatsApp authentication
- Email and password
- Future approved methods

Authentication methods may evolve.

The Smart Business identity of an authorised user remains the same regardless of the authentication method.

# Fair Usage & Sustainability Principle

Smart Business must balance:

- customer freedom
- reliable service quality
- sustainable business operation

Normal business usage should feel unrestricted.

Allowed:

- genuine transactions
- receipts
- business questions
- operational use

Protection required against:

- spam
- non-business abuse
- unlimited unrelated AI conversations
- misuse of storage

Fair usage protects genuine customers.

It should never punish business growth.

# Smart Business Speed Promise

Principle:

Smart Business must feel like a trusted assistant standing beside the user.

Required experience:

- Fast
- Secure
- Reliable

Targets:
- Sub-3-second experience for critical interactions where technically possible
- Secure architecture must never be sacrificed for speed

Architecture principles:
- UUID based isolation
- Strong permission model
- Optimized queries
- Proper indexing
- India-region preference where possible
- Avoid unnecessary middleware delays
