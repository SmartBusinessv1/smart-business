# SMART BUSINESS MISSION CONTROL

# SB-GOV-1.2 — Constitutional Authority Interpretation (Phase 1)

**Decision ID:** SB-GOV-1.2

**Decision Name:** Constitutional Authority Interpretation (Phase 1)

**Status:** FOUNDER APPROVED — ACTIVE GOVERNANCE

**Approved By:** Founder — Riyas PK

**Decision Date:** 2026-07-30

---

## 1. Decision

For Smart Business Phase 1, a separate Smart Business Constitution shall not be created.

Until Phase 1 is completed, the Smart Business constitutional authority shall be interpreted collectively through:

1. `01_Smart_Business_Master_System_Manifesto.md`
2. `11_Smart_Business_Product_Truth_Map.md`

Together, these documents constitute the Smart Business constitutional authority for Phase 1 governance purposes.

---

## 2. Interpretation Rule

Where any approved governance source refers to the **Smart Business Constitution**, that reference shall be interpreted during Phase 1 as referring collectively to:

- `01_Smart_Business_Master_System_Manifesto.md`
- `11_Smart_Business_Product_Truth_Map.md`

Source 01 governs foundational identity, philosophy, mission, locked decisions, and governing principles.

Source 11 governs definitive Product Truth, approved capabilities, product boundaries, roles, permissions, and operational behaviour.

Neither source may override the Lighthouse Constitution.

---

## 3. Rationale

The Founder has determined that:

- the current governance foundation is sufficient to continue Phase 1 implementation;
- creating another large governance document now would slow execution without adding proportional value;
- Smart Business should continue with the approved SB-P missions rather than pause for further constitutional documentation;
- the dedicated Smart Business Constitution should be based on proven implementation experience, operational learning, merchant feedback, and real-world product use.

This decision protects execution velocity while preserving governance clarity.

---

## 4. Phase 1 Governance Effect

This decision resolves the temporary absence of a standalone Smart Business Constitution without changing Product Truth or existing governance authority.

It does not:

- create a new product direction;
- modify approved Phase 1 scope;
- alter permissions, architecture, routes, subscriptions, or AI behaviour;
- authorize specialist rooms to reinterpret governance independently;
- replace the Lighthouse Constitution.

Approved SB-P missions may continue under this interpretation.

---

## 5. Post-Phase 1 Constitutional Mission

After successful completion of Smart Business Phase 1, Mission Control shall initiate a dedicated mission to create:

**Smart Business Constitution v1.0**

The Constitution shall consolidate validated governance derived from:

- Phase 1 implementation experience;
- real-world merchant usage;
- operational findings;
- product and engineering lessons;
- customer feedback;
- proven governance refinements.

Its purpose shall be to consolidate validated constitutional authority, not to invent unsupported governance.

---

## 6. Continuity Rule

Until the dedicated Smart Business Constitution is Founder approved and activated, this decision remains the binding interpretation for every approved reference to the Smart Business Constitution.

Any uncertainty or conflict shall be escalated through Mission Control.

---

## 7. Final Principle

Smart Business governance shall grow from real service, real implementation, and real human experience.

Documentation shall support the mission without delaying useful work.

Human improvement remains the purpose.

---

END OF FOUNDER-APPROVED ACTIVE GOVERNANCE DECISION
