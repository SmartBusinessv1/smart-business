# Purpose

Define how OpenAI models participate in Smart Business.

OpenAI provides reasoning, language understanding and AI assistance.

OpenAI never owns Product Truth, business rules or final business decisions.

# Mission

Deliver trustworthy AI assistance that improves merchant clarity, capability and peace of mind while preserving human ownership of decisions.

# Primary Responsibilities

OpenAI supports:

- Natural language understanding
- Structured information extraction
- Conversation management
- Business insights
- Ask CFO
- Document understanding
- Voice interaction orchestration
- Multilingual support
- Function calling

---

# Boundaries

OpenAI shall not:

- redefine Product Truth
- make financial decisions for merchants
- bypass permissions
- permanently store business records
- invent facts
- replace human judgement

AI assists.

Humans decide.

---

# AI Principles

Preserve:

- AI Assistant, Not AI Judge
- Humans serving humans
- Conversation-first
- Explain reasoning when appropriate
- Ask clarifying questions when uncertain
- Never fabricate information

---

# Prompt Engineering Standards

Prompts shall:

- define objective
- include relevant context
- specify constraints
- require structured outputs where useful
- minimise ambiguity
- avoid unnecessary complexity

---

# Function Calling

Use function/tool calling for:

- database operations
- business actions
- integrations
- calculations
- workflow execution

Never simulate successful actions.

---

# Memory Principles

Business Memory belongs to Smart Business.

OpenAI may use provided context but does not become the permanent system of record.

---

# Model Selection

Choose the smallest capable model that satisfies:

- quality
- latency
- reliability
- cost efficiency

---

# Security

Protect:

- API keys
- merchant information
- customer privacy
- confidential business data

Never expose secrets.

---

# AI Behaviour

The AI shall:

- identify uncertainty
- explain trade-offs
- preserve context
- support founder philosophy
- avoid unsupported claims

- replacing business judgement
