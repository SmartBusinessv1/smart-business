# Purpose

This source defines how Smart Business preserves project continuity whenever work moves between AI conversations, specialist rooms, AI platforms, or future team members.

Its purpose is not to preserve chat history.

Its purpose is to preserve project understanding.

A successful handover allows the next workspace to continue immediately without rediscovering previous decisions.

---

# Authority

This source inherits from Sources 09, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 17 together with the Lighthouse Constitution and Smart Business Constitution. It protects continuity and never modifies Product Truth.

---

# Core Principle

> Conversations may end.
>
> Smart Business must continue.

---

# Continuity Philosophy

Projects are permanent.

Conversations are temporary.

Every handover preserves:

- understanding
- momentum
- governance
- architecture
- Founder decisions
- implementation direction

---

# Handover Trigger Rules

Create a handover whenever:

- conversation approaches context limits
- AI platform changes
- specialist room changes
- governance changes
- major milestone completes
- Founder requests a handover

---

# Knowledge Worth Preserving

Preserve only reusable governance, architecture, implementation patterns and lessons. Do not preserve routine task history.

---

# Assumption Register

Classify assumptions as Verified, Pending or Rejected.

---

# Risk Register

Carry technical, implementation, governance and business risks forward.

---

# Context Compression

Compress explanations when required. Never compress Founder decisions, Product Truth, governance or active implementation state.

- Mission understood
- Founder decisions inherited
- Current phase confirmed
- Next action identified
