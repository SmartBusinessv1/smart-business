## Notes on Numbering

The task brief lists 17 conceptual steps; this runbook implements them as 20 concrete steps because two of the conceptual steps (account recreation, data import) each split into a User-1 branch and a User-2 branch, per the asymmetry finding in `02-authentication-recreation-plan.md` §1 — collapsing them into one step each would have hidden that the two users' migrations happen at genuinely different points in the sequence, which is the single most important scheduling fact this runbook needed to capture correctly.
