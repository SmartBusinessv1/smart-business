# Smart Business Feature Definition — Daily Intelligence Rhythm

**Status:** NOTEBOOKLM SEED — DEEP EXTRACTION REQUIRED  
**Build commitment:** BUILD NOW  
**Commercial availability:** Ledger + Manager core  
**Authority boundary:** Founder-feature elaboration / Product Blueprint input. Not yet implementation-ready.

---

## 1. Feature Identity

Daily Intelligence Rhythm is the scheduled clarity layer that keeps the Owner informed without requiring them to ask.

Its purpose is to reduce mental burden, not create notification overload.

---

## 2. Confirmed Rhythm

The NotebookLM briefing and current Product Truth align on three scheduled touchpoints:

- **7:00 AM — Morning Business Briefing**
- **10:30 AM — Business Pulse Check**
- **10:00 PM — Night Closing Intelligence**

Manager may add richer operational and closing-cash intelligence.

---

## 3. Product Principle

Daily Intelligence should provide timely business clarity using authorised current business data.

It should distinguish:

- facts;
- alerts;
- suggestions;
- unanswered or incomplete data.

It must not create unnecessary fear.

---

## 4. Expected Inputs

Potential shared inputs include, where permitted and implemented:

- Ledger / transactions;
- customer credit;
- Smart Reminder state;
- Stock Intelligence;
- supplier/reorder activity;
- POS / Manager Risk Intelligence;
- Smart Order & Delivery status;
- closing cash information;
- business alerts and unresolved operational exceptions.

The exact composition of each scheduled message still requires deeper NotebookLM extraction.

---

## 5. Channel Experience

The briefing describes automated business updates delivered into WhatsApp.

Current product architecture also requires channel independence, so the underlying intelligence should remain reusable by approved dashboard/conversation surfaces where appropriate.

---

## 6. Owner Authority

Daily Intelligence informs.

It does not automatically make business decisions or punish employees/customers based on signals.

Where it highlights unusual activity, the language should support review rather than accusation.

---
