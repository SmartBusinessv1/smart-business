# Smart Business Feature Definition — Onboarding & First Experience

**Status:** MATURE RECONCILED CONTRACT — FULL HYDRATION PASS  
**Build commitment:** **BUILD NOW**  
**Commercial availability:** Core activation/conversion experience  
**Authority boundary:** Onboarding should create genuine understanding and early merchant value. It must not manipulate through fear, fabricate ROI, or force unresolved trial/pricing assumptions.

---

## 1. Feature Identity

Onboarding & First Experience covers the merchant journey from public `/start` through business setup, product recommendation/activation and the first practical Smart Business wins.

The goal is not to finish a questionnaire. The goal is for the merchant to understand Smart Business and experience useful business clarity quickly.

---

## 2. Founder Problem Statement

A Kerala merchant evaluating Smart Business may not know:

- which product/package fits them;
- what data to provide;
- whether the product will understand their normal language/workflow;
- whether setup will be difficult;
- whether Smart Business produces practical value or merely dashboards/forms.

The first experience must reduce uncertainty and show usefulness without insulting existing habits.

---

## 3. Approved Route and Domain

Current public onboarding entry:

`https://smartbusiness.teamlips.com/start`

`/survey` is deprecated.

Historical domains/subdomains such as `smartbusiness.in`, `audit.smartbusiness.in` and `start.smartbusiness.in` are not current routes.

---

## 4. Lighthouse Principles

- Respect existing merchant habits.
- Ask only what is useful.
- Explain why information is needed.
- Earn trust through practical value.
- No fear-based dependency or inflated ROI promises.
- Let conversation and simple visual steps work together.
- First success should arrive early.
- Preserve English/Malayalam/Manglish choice.

---

## 5. First-stage Discovery

The onboarding journey should understand enough about the merchant to guide setup without becoming a long enterprise form.

Useful discovery may include:

- preferred language;
- business/store identity;
- location/locality;
- business category;
- approximate operating/check-out scale where relevant;
- current recording/POS/spreadsheet habits;
- biggest pain/goal;
- relevant product needs such as stock/staff/order-delivery;
- contact/authentication information required to continue.

Questions should be progressive and justified by the next useful step.

---

## 6. Historical Funnel Evolution — Current Reconciliation

Founder history contains both:

- a simple early three-screen `/start` diagnostic;
- a later deeper multi-screen value-calculator/audit funnel.

Current product truth preserves the **behavioral intent**, not either historical provider/form layout as immutable architecture.

The mature experience should:

- remain simple enough to start;
- gather enough context to give a useful recommendation;
- allow progressive detail rather than forcing unnecessary fields up front;
- support a richer assessment where it materially improves merchant clarity.

---

## 7. Product Recommendation / Trust Result

After sufficient discovery, Smart Business may present a clear recommendation such as:

- Ledger;
- Manager;
- relevant Build Now add-ons where the merchant need indicates them.

The recommendation should explain:

- what problem it addresses;
- why it fits the merchant's stated context;
- what is included;
- what optional add-ons do;
- any unresolved/not-yet-available condition honestly.

Do not hide material pricing/availability information or use manipulative urgency.

---

## 8. Business Identity Setup

The merchant should establish the business identity required for Smart Business, including only the necessary current fields such as:

- business name;
- category/type;
- locality/location information;
- owner/account identity;
- other approved setup fields.

Editing and richer business profile configuration may continue after activation; first-use should not become a long administrative burden.

---

## 9. Existing Data Import

Merchants may already have useful business data in:

- Excel/CSV;
- POS/catalog exports;
- receipts/documents;
- handwritten/paper sources;
- other supported files.

Onboarding should offer appropriate import paths to reduce the empty-workspace problem.

All consequential imports reuse Universal Document Intelligence:

**interpret → preview → clarify where needed → confirm → validated update**.

---

## 10. Activation / Commercial Step

The onboarding experience must connect to the approved Subscription/Payment/Account Lifecycle.

It must not hardcode unresolved commercial policy.

### Trial policy

Historical evidence conflicts between a 14-day full-access trial and later no-free-trial direction. Current Founder decision remains unresolved.

Therefore the mature onboarding contract supports whichever trial/no-trial policy is explicitly approved before launch without requiring a redesign of the core journey.

---

## 11. First Practical Win

The first day should produce concrete usefulness, not merely account creation.

Candidate early wins include:

- record first transaction accurately by text/voice;
- upload/interpret first receipt/document;
- see first useful business summary;
- ask a useful Ask CFO question when available;
- create a reminder;
- import existing data;
- see appropriate Daily Intelligence at the current schedule;
- verify/reconcile a relevant payment where applicable.

The exact sequence should follow feature readiness and merchant context.

---

## 12. First 24-hour Experience

The durable Founder intent is that the merchant should understand the value loop quickly.

The journey may progressively encourage:

1. business/account established;
2. first real business record captured;
3. first document/import or other memory enrichment;
4. first useful retrieval/insight/Ask CFO interaction;
5. first reminder/credit/payment/operational action relevant to the merchant;
6. scheduled Daily Intelligence when the required data/features are available.

Historical old `06:00` Morning Pulse timing is superseded. Current Daily Intelligence schedule is **7:00 AM / 10:30 AM / 10:00 PM**.

---

## 13. Conversation-first Onboarding

Onboarding should demonstrate the product's actual interaction philosophy.

Where appropriate, the merchant should be able to:

- type naturally;
- use voice;
- upload a receipt/file;
- receive plain-language confirmation;
- continue in WhatsApp or the Conversation Workspace.

Do not onboard people through a form-only experience and then reveal an entirely different product later.

---

## 14. Human Language

Language choice should be available early and respected across the journey.

English, Malayalam and Manglish are first-class.

The user should not be forced to restart onboarding because they switch language.

---

## 15. WhatsApp and Conversation Workspace

The merchant may move between approved channels after identity/authentication linkage.

Onboarding must not create separate business identities or duplicate data depending on whether the merchant continues through WhatsApp or the app.

The native Conversation Workspace should be introduced as a first-class alternative, including for times when WhatsApp is unavailable or inconvenient.

---

## 16. Permission / Role Setup

Owner identity is the initial highest business authority.

If staff/manager setup occurs during or after onboarding:

- use explicit invitations/permission assignment;
- do not grant Manager/Employee Owner intelligence by default;
- keep role setup simple enough not to block the Owner's first win.

---

## 17. Support During Onboarding

Onboarding questions should use the Support Automation foundation:

- FAQ-first for common questions;
- English/Malayalam/Manglish;
- AI fallback where appropriate;
- human ticket escalation for unresolved/account-specific issues.

A support issue should not restart completed onboarding state unnecessarily.

---

## 18. Error and Exception Behavior

Handle:

- duplicate account/business attempt;
- authentication/session failure;
- invalid contact information;
- payment failure;
- file/import error;
- ambiguous document interpretation;
- incomplete business identity;
- recommendation data insufficient;
- channel/provider outage;
- feature temporarily unavailable;
- interrupted onboarding/resume.

Preserve completed safe steps and offer a clear recovery path rather than forcing restart.

---

## 19. Privacy and Trust

- Ask only necessary information.
- Explain purpose for sensitive fields.
- Do not collect sensitive employee KYC as a default onboarding requirement while that product decision remains unresolved.
- Do not use onboarding data for unrelated hidden manipulation.
- Merchant data remains business-scoped.
- Support access follows purpose-limited governance.

---

## 20. Performance and Simplicity

The first experience should feel fast and calm.

Use progressive loading/setup and clear progress where necessary. Avoid long blocking workflows when independent setup can continue.

Do not weaken correctness/security merely to reduce clicks.

---

## 21. Shared Foundations to Reuse

Reuse:

- authentication/business identity;
- Human Language Layer;
- Conversation Workspace/WhatsApp;
- Universal Document Intelligence;
- Ledger;
- Ask CFO;
- Reminder Engine;
- Daily Intelligence;
- Subscription/Entitlement lifecycle;
- Support Automation;
- Permission Engine;
- audit/idempotency.

---

## 22. Explicit Non-goals

- `/survey` as current route;
- historical Typeform/Fillout/Make.com as permanent architecture;
- fear-based or misleading ROI conversion tactics;
- fixed old domain references;
- hardcoded trial assumption before Founder decision;
- forcing all merchant setup before any practical win;
- treating historical 250-merchant cap as current default policy without reactivation.

---

## 23. Acceptance Scenarios

A future Blueprint/EIS must verify at least:

1. Merchant reaches current `/start` and selects/uses preferred language.
2. Minimal discovery yields an understandable product recommendation.
3. Business identity is created once and persists across channels.
4. Existing CSV/document import uses preview/confirmation.
5. First text/voice transaction produces correct confirmation.
6. Merchant can resume interrupted onboarding safely.
7. Payment failure does not destroy completed setup/data.
8. Trial/no-trial behavior is driven by explicit current commercial policy.
9. First-day value is demonstrated through at least one real business workflow.
10. Current Daily Intelligence times are used, not historical 06:00/09:15.
11. Staff setup never grants Owner intelligence by default.
12. Support escalation preserves onboarding continuity.
