## 1. Feature Identity

Smart Business Ledger is the core **AI Business Memory and Daily Clarity System**.

It exists so a merchant can record and retrieve business activity naturally instead of becoming a software operator.

The product experience is conversation-first and should support ordinary merchant habits such as speaking, typing, sending a receipt/photo or asking later what happened.

---

## 2. Founder Problem Statement

Kerala merchants may manage business information through notebooks, memory, WhatsApp notes, paper bills, Excel or POS systems.

Smart Business should not insult or replace those habits by force.

It should convert business activity into structured, searchable memory while reducing mental load.

## 4. Core Interaction Model

The canonical early heartbeat remains:

Merchant sends a natural business statement such as an expense or income.

Smart Business should:

1. understand the business meaning;
2. identify the intended action;
3. ask clarification when confidence is insufficient;
4. create or prepare the correct structured business record;
5. confirm what was actually saved;
6. make the information available to later search, reporting and intelligence.

---

## 5. Users

### Owner

Uses Ledger as full business memory and receives owner intelligence.

### Manager

May use permitted operational capabilities according to Owner delegation.

### Employee

May add permitted transactions and upload permitted documents where authorized.

Employees do not receive Owner financial intelligence by default.

---

## 6. Channels

Expected channels include:

- WhatsApp text;
- WhatsApp voice;
- photos/documents;
- Smart Business dashboard/app entry where approved;
- Conversation Workspace as another channel over shared Business Memory.

Business logic must not be duplicated per channel.

## 7. Shared Foundations

Ledger should be treated as a foundation reused by:

- Ask CFO;
- Daily Intelligence;
- Smart Credit Awareness;
- Receipt Intelligence;
- Payment Verification;
- reports/exports;
- reminders where linked to transactions;
- operational features that need business history.

---

## 8. Permission Boundary

Business data remains tenant-isolated.

Frontend visibility is not authorization.

Employee contribution does not grant employee access to profit, owner analytics or complete business intelligence.

---

## 9. AI Behaviour

AI must:

- classify intent before assuming every message is a transaction;
- ask when uncertain;
- never fabricate amount, party, purpose or transaction type;
- distinguish user correction from new transaction where possible;
- preserve human decision ownership.
