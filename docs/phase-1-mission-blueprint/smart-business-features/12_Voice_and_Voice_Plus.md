# Basic Voice Assistant & Smart Voice Assistant Plus

## Feature Identity

**Basic Voice Assistant:** Ledger + Manager core, **BUILD NOW**.  
**Smart Voice Assistant Plus:** Ledger + Manager add-on, **BUILD NOW**.

## Founder Problem Statement

Busy merchants often cannot stop serving customers to type. Voice should let them record, ask, clarify and continue work naturally without turning every interaction into long audio.

## Basic Voice

Basic Voice supports approved core interactions such as:

- transaction/credit/repayment input;
- Ask CFO questions;
- reminder creation;
- stock/order/operational instructions where the user's product and permissions allow;
- short confirmations and useful short responses.

Voice input must pass through the same intent, permission, safety, clarification and confirmation rules as text.

## Voice Plus

Voice Plus adds deeper conversational depth, including:

- richer multi-turn discussion;
- deeper Ask CFO explanation;
- follow-up context;
- structured summaries;
- action-item extraction;
- continuation into reminders or other owner-approved workflows.

Premium depth does not mean unnecessarily long audio.

## Language and Personality

Support English, Malayalam and Manglish. Voice should feel calm, respectful, simple and locally natural rather than robotic or broadcast-formal.

## Output Design

- Routine confirmations should be short.
- Dense tables and exact figures should use concise audio plus readable text where useful.
- Long/deep audio should be user-driven or clearly justified by the task.
- Sensitive Owner intelligence should not be spoken to unauthorized roles or in an unsuitable public context.

## Permissions

Voice does not create authority. Each action/output inherits the user's role and the underlying feature permission.

Employees do not receive Owner profit/Ask CFO/full analytics by default. Suppliers/customers/delivery staff receive only bounded workflow communication.

## Confirmation and Ambiguity

Unclear voice must trigger clarification rather than guessing. Consequential writes/actions require the same confirmation model as equivalent text input.

## Error Behaviour

When transcription or generation fails:

- preserve the user's input safely where appropriate;
- say what failed without pretending success;
- offer the quickest alternative, commonly text/retry;
- do not create records from uncertain interpretation.

## Privacy

Audio retention and transcription must follow current privacy/retention policy. Do not use voice as a route around role restrictions.

## Historical Corrections

Superseded: the old assumption that all useful outbound voice requires a separate voice add-on. Current Product Truth includes Basic Voice in Ledger and Manager; Voice Plus is the premium depth layer.

Historical provider names, exact tuning weights and fixed duration targets remain implementation detail.
