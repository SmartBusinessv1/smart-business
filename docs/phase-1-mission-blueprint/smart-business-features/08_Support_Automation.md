# Smart Business Feature Definition — Support Automation

**Status:** NOTEBOOKLM SEED — DEEP EXTRACTION REQUIRED  
**Build commitment:** BUILD NOW  
**Commercial availability:** Core support foundation  
**Authority boundary:** Founder-feature elaboration / Product Blueprint input. Not yet implementation-ready.

---

## 1. Feature Identity

Support Automation is the Smart Business support layer designed to resolve common merchant questions quickly, clearly and at sustainable operating cost.

The support principle is:

**FAQ first → AI support when needed → ticket/escalation when unresolved.**

---

## 2. Confirmed Seed Capabilities

NotebookLM briefing and current support sources support:

- 100+ FAQ Knowledge Base target;
- English support;
- Malayalam support;
- Manglish support;
- FAQ matching before expensive AI reasoning;
- AI escalation for questions that require contextual reasoning;
- support request/ticket creation when unresolved;
- normal business operation should continue even when support is unresolved.

---

## 3. Support Philosophy

Support should reduce confusion before it becomes a larger problem.

It must preserve:

- merchant trust;
- privacy;
- clear authority boundaries;
- honest uncertainty;
- respectful language;
- sustainable solo-founder/lean-team operations.

---

## 4. Privacy Seed

Routine Team LIPS operations should not depend on staff casually viewing individual merchant business information.

If account-specific support requires inspecting private information, the support workflow must respect the approved consent / mutual-agreement boundary and purpose limitation.

The deeper NotebookLM pass must recover the exact historical wording and escalation workflow.

---

## 5. Channel Seed

Support should work primarily through the same approved conversational surfaces merchants already use, including WhatsApp and dashboard/conversation experiences where supported.

Support state should not silently interrupt Ledger/bookkeeping operations.
