## Purpose

Connect an authenticated user to a durable Smart Business business identity so later business data and workspace behavior could belong to a defined merchant/business context.

## Historical scope

The mission established the early foundation around:

- business schema / business record;
- onboarding/business identity creation;
- the relationship between authenticated user and business workspace;
- the first meaningful durable business-data foundation.

## Why it mattered

Authentication answers who the user is. Smart Business also needed to know which business the user owns or belongs to before protected business data could be safely created and isolated.

## What changed

The application moved from generic authenticated access into a merchant/business-aware tenancy model.

## Capability gained

Team LIPS gained the foundation for business ownership, business-scoped data and later row-level isolation.

## Key lesson

User identity is not business identity. Business ownership and business isolation require their own explicit model.
