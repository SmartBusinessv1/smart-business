## Historical scope

- email/password authentication;
- managed Google OAuth where enabled;

- session persistence/restoration;
- sign-out;

## Why it mattered

Protected merchant data and business functionality required a reliable user-access boundary first.

## What changed

Smart Business moved from a public/application shell into an authenticated application experience.

## Capability gained

Team LIPS gained an application-access foundation that later business identity and business data could safely depend on.

## Key lesson

Authentication, business identity and business authorization are separate layers. Successful sign-in does not by itself establish merchant ownership or access to business intelligence.
