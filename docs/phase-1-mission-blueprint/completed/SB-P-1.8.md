## Purpose

Introduce the first substantial owner-scoped business transaction operations while preserving business isolation and avoiding premature expansion into the wider Smart Business finance/intelligence vision.

## Historical scope

The mission established the early transaction foundation around:

- owner-scoped sales entry;
- owner-scoped purchase entry;
- transaction timeline;
- dashboard totals;
- transaction schema;
- Row Level Security / business isolation;
- append-oriented operational behavior.

The initial implementation required correction and independent re-review before closure.

## Why it mattered

Smart Business needed to move beyond workspace scaffolding into useful daily business recording while preserving trust between merchants and their data.

## What changed

The product gained real business operations rather than placeholder UI.

## Capability gained

Team LIPS gained practical experience implementing and verifying owner-scoped financial/operational data under RLS and multi-business isolation constraints.

## Key lesson

A working form and dashboard do not prove a safe business system. Business isolation, effective authorization and runtime behavior require separate evidence.
