## Purpose in the historical sequence

Smart Business first needed an operating system for coordinated execution before product implementation could proceed safely.

The Command Foundation established the early project structure around:

- the Smart Business ChatGPT Project HQ;
- the initial shared source stack;
- Founder Room;
- specialist rooms and Labs;
- Founder-directed room calibration;
- Source 09 / roadmap-state command coordination;
- Mission Control activation;
- mission routing, sequencing and handover.

## What changed

Smart Business moved from largely Founder-plus-AI conversation into a coordinated multi-room operating model with shared context and visible authority.

The project gained a practical distinction between:

- Founder authority;
- Mission Control coordination;
- specialist capability;
- source/governance context;
- mission-scoped execution.

## Why it mattered

Specialist intelligence alone was not enough. Without shared state, sequencing, handover and authority boundaries, parallel AI-assisted work could fragment or drift.

The Command Foundation provided the coordination layer required for later Product Missions.

## Capability gained

Team LIPS gained an early reusable capability to coordinate multiple AI/specialist workstreams under Founder authority while maintaining shared project context.
