## Historical scope

The mission verified evidence around:

- control of `teamlips.com`;

## Why it mattered

Smart Business needed a dedicated product domain, but infrastructure changes could not safely begin from assumptions. The mission established the prove-before-change pattern that later became a recurring Team LIPS operating lesson.

## What changed

The project moved from assumed domain availability to evidenced infrastructure control.

Mission Control could therefore authorize the next domain step without guessing ownership or current configuration.

## Capability gained

Team LIPS gained evidence-first domain/DNS operating discipline:

- inspect current state;
- preserve unrelated services;
- identify exact provider requirements;
- change only after the target state is understood.

## Key lesson

Missing provider configuration or uncertain infrastructure state is an evidence gap, not permission to improvise.
