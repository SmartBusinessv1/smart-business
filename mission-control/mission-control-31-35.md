## Institutional synthesis: what future Mission Control must inherit

1. **Keep the human/product purpose above paperwork.** Move through genuine acceptance gates efficiently and avoid non-blocking provider reply delays.
2. **Documentary readiness ≠ code ≠ runtime proof.** Blueprint/EIS/package locks do not grant migrations, production, commercial pilot or implementation.
3. **The owner of a shared business capability remains accountable.** SB-P-1.14 records and SB-P-1.16 financial balances, SB-P-1.15 Daily Intelligence and SB-P-1.19 onboarding, and Kernel permission foundations must not be silently duplicated.
4. **Exact legal/privacy unknowns stay explicit.** Never invent retention durations, payroll rates, legal bases or automatic authority.
5. **Independent evidence must genuinely differ from authored evidence.** A script derived from the author's own FCTM is not sufficient independent source coverage; a review of an old EIS blob cannot approve a corrected one.
6. **Preserve a minimal approved correction.** Fix administrative path errors byte-for-byte, review the exact changed delta, and do not reopen an entire accepted stage for one narrow defect.
7. **Domain expertise does not automatically grant approval authority.** Inspect canonical appointment, source ownership and delegation records before demanding another archived-room signature.
8. **Protect Owner financial intelligence and human control.** Ask CFO stays read-only; manager delegation stays specific; AI interpretation and voice/provider paths are subordinate to governed commands.
9. **No source or room identity should be invented.** Founder room labels and internal MC decision IDs are different provenance classes; mark unverified original titles clearly.
10. **Always supply the Founder the next eligible actor and executable handoff.** Identity, destination, task, exact authority, evidence and return path should be explicit.
11. **Reuse prior reviews and preserve historic evidence provenance.** Later canonical repairs may close old blockers. Historical guides are not live-to-do lists.
12. **Use one current authoritative baseline.** Before any action, re-read current `merge/active/`, `mission-control/mission_memory.md`, active mission stage records, current PR head and any relevant authorized provider/runtime facts.
