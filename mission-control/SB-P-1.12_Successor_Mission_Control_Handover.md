**Product outcome:** Smart Business knows which human is acting, to which business they belong, what authority the Owner has delegated and what data/actions are protected; merchants encounter a coherent Product & Price Master rather than a competing software category.

Required areas to reconcile from the **complete primary contracts**, not merely the Build Plan summary:

- Business isolation/cross-tenant denial in UI, APIs, exports, data layer and future shared action access.
- RLS, table grants, privileged functions, residual `anon` grants and security evidence.
- Owner financial intelligence protected by default from employees/managers absent approved explicit capability.

- Product & Price Master reclassification; contextual, role-aware product-price-inventory experience.
- Safe `/catalog` contextualization/demotion **without losing valid product/pricing/inventory data or breaking existing deep-link continuity before replacement access is proven**.

**Founder-reserved runtime scenarios, already approved:**

A. Owner grants a Manager bounded contextual product-price-inventory access; Manager sees only permitted operational areas, Owner-only financial surfaces remain denied; prove UI **and backend/RLS denial**.

B. Manager starts a consequential preview (e.g., Product & Price Master bulk import); Owner revokes relevant permission before commit; backend refuses commit and no protected write occurs.
