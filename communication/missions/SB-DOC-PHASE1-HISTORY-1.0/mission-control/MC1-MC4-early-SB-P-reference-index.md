# SB-DOC-PHASE1-HISTORY-1.0 — MC1–MC4 Early SB-P Reference Index

## Purpose

This index prevents later Mission Control rooms from being treated only as evidence about their own immediate period.

MC1–MC4 contain retrospective, dependency and repository-history references to earlier Product Missions. Those references are useful evidence for reconstructing the full `SB-P-1.0` through `SB-P-1.11` sequence, even though MC1–MC4 themselves are not the earliest Smart Business chats.

This index does not claim that MC1–MC4 contain the complete history of any earlier mission.

## Current Evidence Boundary

The complete early mission chain is not yet established.

Current evidence from MC1–MC4 and the repository strongly indicates a product-foundation progression including:

- Application Access Foundation;
- Business Identity Foundation;
- Business Workspace Foundation;
- Business Operations Foundation;
- transaction workflow refinement;
- Inventory Foundation;
- Product Catalog & Pricing.

Historical repository records also identify early mission identifiers using both older and newer formatting, including `SB-P1.5`, `SB-P1.6`, `SB-P1.7`, `SB-P-1.8`, `SB-P-1.9`, `SB-P-1.10`, and `SB-P-1.11`.

The reconstruction must not infer the missing `SB-P-1.0` through `SB-P-1.4` names merely from sequence position. Those identities remain evidence targets.

## Extraction Rule For Future Batches

For every uploaded Mission Control or specialist-room batch:

1. Extract the room's direct current-period events.
2. Search the entire batch for references to any mission from `SB-P-1.0` through `SB-P-1.11`.
3. Classify each reference as one of:
   - contemporaneous mission evidence;
   - retrospective evidence;
   - dependency evidence;
   - later correction/supersession evidence;
   - unresolved inference.
4. Corroborate with repository history where possible.
5. Update the mission register progressively without inventing missing details.

## Completed-Folder Rule

The final reconstructed records for missions that predate the modern Blueprint lifecycle must not impersonate contemporaneous Product Blueprints.

When created under `docs/phase-1-mission-blueprint/completed/`, each such record must state:

`Document Type: Historical Mission Continuity Record — NOT A RETROACTIVE PRODUCT BLUEPRINT`

This allows the folder to serve as the continuous Phase 1 mission-history index while preserving historical truth.
