# 12. Lessons Learned

## 12.1 Do not overload always-loaded AI context

**Situation:** Smart Business had governance, Product Truth, repository instructions, and platform context that could all have been copied into Project Knowledge.

**Learning:** Keep Project Knowledge concise and persistent; keep detailed implementation truth in the repository.

**Effect:** Reduced duplication, drift, and maintenance burden.

**Classification:** Organization-wide capability candidate.

## 12.2 Verify platform behaviour before designing governance around it

Lovable Knowledge, Skills, Plan Mode, Build Mode, GitHub integration, and Supabase integration were investigated before being assigned permanent roles.

**Learning:** Platform assumptions should follow observed behaviour, not precede it.

## 12.3 Build then observe before correcting

Bootstrap validation deliberately required the Founder to capture the first generated output before fixing visible defects.

**Learning:** First-run defects are evidence about prompt/context quality. Immediate correction destroys diagnostic evidence.

## 12.4 Authentication before business functionality

SB-P1.5 established access/session/protected routes before deeper domain functionality.

**Learning:** Security and identity boundaries should precede protected business capability.

## 12.5 Separate user identity, business identity, workspace, and operations

The executed sequence became:

`Application Access → Business Identity → Business Workspace → Business Operations`.

**Learning:** Progressive foundations produce clearer evidence and lower coupling than trying to build the whole merchant cockpit at once.

## 12.6 Do not simulate unfinished product capability

SB-P1.7 explicitly used non-functional Coming Soon cards and prohibited fake metrics, fake transactions, fake AI answers, and fake notifications.

**Learning:** A foundation UI may explain future capability without pretending it exists.

## 12.7 Connection success is not backend identity proof

The Supabase/Lovable investigation showed that OAuth success did not prove the external Supabase project was the actual project backend.

**Learning:** Verify exact runtime/config identity before architecture conclusions.

## 12.8 Preserve evidence gaps honestly

SB-P-1.8 evidence work explicitly recorded evidence that could no longer be reproduced safely instead of rolling back state to recreate screenshots/stdout.

**Learning:** Missing historical evidence is better than destructive evidence fabrication.
