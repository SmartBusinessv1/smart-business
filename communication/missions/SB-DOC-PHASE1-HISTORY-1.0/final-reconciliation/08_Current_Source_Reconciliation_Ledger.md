# 1. Authority Boundary

This ledger is an **interpretation and escalation control**, not a constitutional amendment.

It does not:

- rewrite Source 01;
- rewrite Source 11;
- create new Founder decisions;
- convert historical implementation detail into Product Truth;
- authorize engineering to choose among conflicting product meanings;
- treat a mature feature contract as permission to contradict Source 01 or Source 11.

Where Source 01/11 are clear, they remain controlling.

Where mature feature contracts add implementation-neutral depth without contradiction, no constitutional source change is required merely for completeness.

Where current source wording is materially stale, compressed, ambiguous, or inconsistent with a later Founder-approved direction, this ledger records that fact and routes any canonical amendment through a separate Founder/Mission Control governance action.

Operating rule:

> **Historical recovery may expose source drift. It may not silently repair constitutional Product Truth.**

# 2. Reconciliation Dispositions

Every source-level finding in this ledger uses one of these dispositions.

## `NO SOURCE CHANGE REQUIRED`

Source 01/11 already express the controlling product truth. The mature contract may add useful workflow depth without changing the constitutional meaning.

## `SOURCE CLARIFICATION RECOMMENDED`

The current source is not materially wrong, but its wording is compressed, incomplete, or likely to create future implementation drift. A clarification may be proposed through proper governance.

## `SOURCE AMENDMENT REQUIRED — FOUNDER / MISSION CONTROL AUTHORIZATION NEEDED`

Current source wording materially conflicts with a later controlling Founder decision or approved product truth. No canonical edit may occur through this historical mission alone.

## `HISTORICAL ONLY — DO NOT PROPAGATE`

The recovered behavior is provenance, an old implementation assumption, or a superseded mechanism and must not be pushed into Source 01/11.

## `UNRESOLVED FOUNDER DECISION — NO SOURCE CHANGE YET`

The evidence does not safely establish a present product decision. Preserve the question and do not amend constitutional Product Truth until the Founder decides.

1. identify the exact current source text;

5. obtain Founder authority where required;
6. update all dependent mature contracts/registers only after the canonical source change is accepted;
7. preserve historical provenance rather than deleting the prior interpretation.
