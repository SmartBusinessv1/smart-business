## 8. Organizational Lesson

The source evolution carries two equally important lessons.

### Lesson A — Under-governance creates ambiguity

The initial nine-source brain was sufficient to define product, architecture, trust and brand direction, but execution later exposed lifecycle, security, environment, multi-agent, evidence and continuity problems that required additional structure.

### Lesson B — Over-distributed governance creates friction

The later 25-file system preserved more truth but created a larger active authority surface. Consolidation was needed so governance could remain strong without forcing builders or Mission Control to carry unnecessary document complexity.

The mature learning is:

> Governance should expand when execution proves a real control gap, then consolidate when equivalent truths can be carried more clearly by fewer active authorities.

This aligns with the later Mission Control doctrine:

> **Maximum clarity, minimum necessary ceremony.**
