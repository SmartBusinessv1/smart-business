## 2. Customer Success and Strategic Partnerships / People become explicit rooms

The planning discussion adds two responsibilities to the Smart Business room system:

- Customer Success;
- Strategic Partnerships / People, including early hiring.

### Customer Success historical intent

Customer Success is framed as the function that tests whether the merchant actually receives the value promised by Product, Brand, and Onboarding.

Historical responsibilities include:

- first-hour / first-day / first-week / first-month activation;
- first successful transaction;
- first saved receipt;
- first reminder;
- first useful Ask CFO interaction;
- customer-health monitoring;
- confusion detection;
- satisfaction and renewal confidence;
- feedback, testimonials, referrals, and churn prevention.

The central historical question is whether the merchant becomes more confident, less stressed, and more in control.

This is strongly aligned with the later canonical Smart Business promise and current Customer Success / Support framing, but the planning-era room prompt remains historical operating lineage rather than current room authority.

### Strategic Partnerships / People historical intent

The Founder explicitly asks that hiring be included with strategic partnerships at this stage.

The rationale is that early hiring is not treated as a mature corporate HR department problem. It is treated as a strategic question of:

- who should join;
- when they should join;
- why they are needed;
- under what relationship;
- whether the person or partner strengthens or distracts from the mission.

The room also covers:

- partners;
- investors;
- advisors;
- merchant associations;
- accountants;
- distributors;
- channel relationships;
- collaborations;
- ecosystem development.

The planning boundary is clear: the room researches and prepares; Founder authority decides consequential partnership, investment, and people decisions.

## 3. Smart Business HQ becomes a reusable Lighthouse project-creation method

A major historical development in Part 15 is the recognition that the Smart Business ChatGPT Project structure is not only a one-off SaaS workspace.

The planning dialogue generalizes the method into a reusable organizational pattern:

- create a foundation / constitutional source;
- define decision rooms;
- define execution rooms;
- create labs where necessary;
- create a roadmap command;
- activate Mission Control;
- work through milestones with proof.

The discussion deliberately rejects copying the full Smart Business room set into every future project.

Examples are explored for:

- Lighthouse parent organization;
- Team LIPS;
- LIPS Learners;
- future websites, courses, tools, services, marketplaces, physical products, and internal systems.

The enduring lesson is **structure should match mission complexity**.

Smart Business is a reference case, not a universal template.

## 4. Lighthouse Project Architect concept

Part 15 develops a historical `Lighthouse Project Architect` concept: an AI-assisted project architect intended to convert ideas into structured project operating systems.

Its proposed knowledge stack includes:

- Lighthouse Constitution — why;
- Project Architect SOP — how to create;
- Smart Business Case Study — reference experience;
- Lighthouse Capability Library — what Lighthouse already owns, knows, and has learned.

### Discovery-before-architecture rule

The Architect is instructed not to immediately create source files, rooms, roadmaps, or implementation plans.

Instead, it should:

1. understand the idea;
2. summarize its current understanding;
3. ask a small number of useful questions;
4. think with the Founder rather than merely collect answers;
5. identify hidden opportunities, risks, alternatives, and simpler paths;
6. understand ecosystem, stakeholders, current assets, and future vision;
7. obtain a scope-confirmation gate before final project architecture.

### Existing-source awareness

A particularly useful correction arises when testing the Architect on a Lighthouse parent website.

The Founder points out that when the AI already has the Lighthouse Constitution, it should not ask the Founder to redefine Lighthouse from scratch.

The resulting rule is:

- distinguish **Known Foundation** from uploaded sources;
- distinguish **Unknown Project Decisions** requiring Founder discovery;
- use existing source truth first;
- ask only what is genuinely unresolved.

This is an important ancestor of later repository-first and source-first AI governance.

## 5. Capability Memory — organizational knowledge should compound

One of Part 15's strongest organizational ideas is the Founder's request for **resources memory and skill memory**.

The Founder gives practical examples:

- Lighthouse may already have Hostinger capacity for many WordPress sites;
- Canva may already satisfy poster/design needs;
- Claude may already be available for software work;
- WordPress experience gained in one project should inform the next website project;
- Smart Business should leave reusable SaaS-building capability for future Team LIPS products.

The planning response develops `03_Lighthouse_Capability_Library.md` as a historical institutional-memory concept.

Its proposed categories include:

- infrastructure assets;
- software and subscriptions;
- skill memory;
- build experience;
- marketing capability;
- partner / network memory;
- lessons learned.

The intended operating rule is to separate future project needs into:

- Already Available;
- Existing Skills;
- Need to Acquire;
- Need to Learn;
- Need to Outsource.

The purpose is not to assume all past capability remains sufficient. The purpose is to avoid unnecessary spending, duplicated tooling, repeated relearning, and organizational amnesia.

## 6. Lessons Learned become reusable organizational assets

Part 15 explicitly connects lessons learned to capability memory.

The proposed distinction is:

- Case Study = what happened;
- Capability Library = what Lighthouse gained and can reuse.

Example planning-era lesson:

- do not begin marketing before identity/infrastructure verification when those are dependencies.

The larger institutional insight is that a project should increase the capability of the organization that created it.

Smart Business is therefore framed not only as a product being built, but also as a school through which Lighthouse / Team LIPS gains reusable operating capability.

## 7. Project feasibility and resource realism

The Project Architect SOP is expanded to cover feasibility before finalization.

Planning-era feasibility areas include:

- realistic time estimates;
- one-time and recurring cost;
- existing asset adjustment;
- required tools;
- human requirements;
- complexity scoring;
- risk review;
- physical-product requirements where applicable;
- START NOW / SIMPLIFY FIRST / WAIT / REJECT recommendation.

For physical products, the discussion adds checks for:

- manufacturing / production method;
- suppliers and raw materials;
- MOQ;
- packaging;
- quality control;
- storage and workspace;
- distribution;
- registrations / certifications / legal needs;
- realistic full cost.

This reinforces a broader Founder pattern seen earlier: **do not equate technical possibility with responsible creation**.

## 8. Project Finalization Mode

The historical Architect workflow is patched so that, after sufficient discovery and Founder approval, it does not stop at recommendations.

It should enter a finalization stage and produce:

1. source files;
2. room activation prompts;
3. roadmap / Mission Control material;

The report is intended to carry:

- project identity;
- Lighthouse alignment;
- target users;
- recommended sources;
- recommended rooms;
- tools;
- cost;
- timeline;
- complexity;
- risk;
- roadmap;
- Mission Control starting point.

This is a historical precursor to later formal mission artifacts, Founder Briefs, staged handovers, and repository-first continuity.

## 9. Claude Pro / Claude CTO knowledge-stack design

Part 15 also returns to the engineering environment and asks what Claude Pro actually needs to know.

The planning discussion deliberately argues against uploading every company source into the engineering environment.

The historical principle is **minimum sufficient context for the assigned engineering responsibility**.

The proposed stack evolves through several corrections and ends around a focused technical knowledge set containing variants of:

- Lighthouse Constitution;
- Smart Business Master System Manifesto;
- system architecture;
- API / WhatsApp / OpenAI framework;
- database / Supabase security framework;
- Lovable build framework;
- Claude CTO build roadmap.

The planning objective is to stop Claude from inventing unrelated product or marketing work when the active engineering milestone is narrow.

### Important current-governance boundary

These planning-era Claude role diagrams and source-stack names are historical lineage only.

Current Source 17 and Source 18 supersede this old operating model with explicit Mission Control authority, repository-first evidence, Codex / Claude Code stage ownership, specialist review, locked artifacts, independent verification, and no self-approval.

The historical lesson survives:

> give an execution AI the authoritative context required for its responsibility, but not unrelated context that encourages scope drift.
