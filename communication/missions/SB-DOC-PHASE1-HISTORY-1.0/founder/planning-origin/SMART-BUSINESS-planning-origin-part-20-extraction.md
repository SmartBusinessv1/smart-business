## 5.1 Founder distinction: Lighthouse Constitution vs execution operating system

The Founder explicitly describes the Lighthouse Constitution as the universal human foundation — the `blood and fuel` of future projects — while describing Lighthouse Architect as execution-oriented, focused on:

- productivity;
- quality;
- accountability;
- Founder Vision protection;
- end-user experience;
- disciplined handover;
- continuous learning.

This is a major organizational distinction:

**Lighthouse Constitution answers WHY and protects humans.**

**Architect / project operating systems answer HOW work is executed well.**

That distinction remains valuable even though the exact later Lighthouse Architect file architecture is outside Smart Business governance.

---

## 5.2 Founder accountability and quality accountability

The Founder adds two accountability dimensions:

1. **Execution/productivity accountability** — aligned with `do not delay; do not rush`; every participant knows responsibility, timing, boundaries, and completion criteria.
2. **Quality/context accountability** — long-running rooms should not expand indefinitely until context quality degrades.

This produces the planning-era Controlled Handover model:

`Entry Criteria → Responsibilities → Completion Criteria → Handover Package → Exit`.

The Founder specifically identifies horizontal handover within one department — for example Room 1 handing to Room 2 — as a way to maintain quality rather than continuously bloating one chat.

This later strongly echoes current repository-first continuity and Source 18 handover requirements.
