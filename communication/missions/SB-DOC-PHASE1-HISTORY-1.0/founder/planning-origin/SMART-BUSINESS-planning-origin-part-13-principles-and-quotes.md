## 2. Founder Decisions Preserved

### Historical memory must not compete with current authority

**FOUNDER DECISION / ACCEPTED OPERATING MODEL**

The original long planning thread becomes `00_Founding_Archive`, while current work moves into fresh specialist rooms governed by reviewed Project Sources.

This preserves history without allowing old ideas, superseded prompts or earlier mistakes to dominate current execution.

**CURRENT CANONICAL ECHO:** Source 17 later states that individual conversations are temporary and that project understanding must be preserved through approved governance and durable operational records.

---

### Specialist rooms should have distinct responsibilities

**FOUNDER DIRECT / DECISION**

The Founder explicitly wants separate chats because each room holds different operations.

Examples created in Part 13 include Founder strategy, CTO engineering, Lovable, Supabase, AI/WhatsApp, Brand, Legal/Admin and Research.

The Founder also prefers separate practical / Lab spaces so screenshots, errors and experiments do not clutter the main decision discussion.

**ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE:**

> `Department chat decides. Lab chat experiments.`

This is not current governance wording, but it is an important early containment idea.

---

### Source absorption should be verified, not assumed

**FOUNDER DECISION**

The Founder repeatedly asks that every newly created room be verified as having fully absorbed the latest sources before moving on.

This creates a planning-era calibration discipline:

`create room → load Sources 00–08 → request proof of understanding → review response → activate next room`.

**CURRENT CANONICAL ECHO:** Later governance requires mandatory source loading, mission interpretation, explicit scope and evidence before work.

---

### Expert identity should be paired with project identity

**FOUNDER DIRECT**

The Founder asks whether each room should begin with expert framing such as an expert Lovable SaaS creator with Supabase experience.

The accepted structure becomes:

`Expert Identity + Room Activation + Source Alignment + Responsibilities + Boundaries + Confirmation Request`.

Historical significance: the Founder is not trying to create generic experts; each expert must first become a Smart Business specialist constrained by project truth.

## 4. Lab Containment Principle

**ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE**

**FOUNDER DECISION / ACCEPTED ORGANIZATIONAL GAP**

Finance & Payments is created to own banking, payment systems, subscription operations, invoices, reconciliation and financial administration.

## 8. Customer Success Insight

The planning co-founder identifies a missing distinction between product UX/support and customer success.

**ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE:**

Support asks:

> `Customer has a problem. How do we fix it?`

Customer Success asks:

> `Why did the customer reach this problem in the first place?`

Brand asks:

> `How do we convince merchants?`

Customer Success asks:

> `After joining, did we prove our promise?`

The proposed success question is whether merchants actually experience the promised clarity, reduced stress and control after activation.

This is historically significant even though room naming / authority later evolves.

## 9. Founder Identifies Mission Control as the Playmaker

This is the most important Founder-origin principle in Part 13.

**FOUNDER DIRECT**

The Founder says the heart of the chat system should be a dedicated chat that:

- moves the project in the right direction;
- helps choose the correct chat;
- helps when the Founder is stuck;
- holds the roadmap;
- knows where to start and what comes first;
- understands the digital handshakes from beginning to launch;
- verifies milestones;
- follows progress from subdomain connection to first client WhatsApp transaction;
- continues through 10, 100 and 500 clients.

The Founder describes this role as:

> `this is the play maker in our system`

**FOUNDER DECISION / ORGANIZATIONAL INTENT:**

This is not another specialist department. It is a coordinating layer above departments.

**CURRENT CANONICAL ECHO:** Current Mission Control owns sequencing, coordination, evidence gates, room activation, acceptance and closure under approved governance.

## 10. Source-Set Philosophy

Part 13 inherits the Part 12 belief that a small reviewed source set is better than uncontrolled raw-document accumulation.

**ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE:**

> `8–9 excellent sources beat 50 documents.`

The initial operating behavior validates this: every new room is hydrated from the same nine reviewed Sources `00–08`, rather than from the entire raw planning history.

Later project history demonstrates the other side of the learning cycle: governance expanded as execution exposed real needs, then was consolidated again.

**Historical maturity arc:**

`small reviewed source foundation`

→ `execution creates new governance needs`

→ `source set expands`

→ `governance becomes too distributed / difficult to maintain`

→ `consolidation preserves truth with fewer active authorities`.
