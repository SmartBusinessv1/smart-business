## 1. Provenance rule

Part 20 mixes direct Founder statements with planning-assistant formulations.

This file separates:

- **FOUNDER DIRECT** — wording or decision directly attributable to the Founder in the source;
- **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE** — useful formulation created by the planning assistant;
- **CURRENT CANONICAL ECHO** — a concept later independently preserved in approved Smart Business sources.

No assistant-created phrase becomes a Founder quote or governance rule merely because it is memorable.

# 2. Founder-rooted principles

## P20-F01 — Lighthouse Constitution is the universal human foundation

**Class:** FOUNDER DIRECT — concept

The Founder describes the Lighthouse Constitution as universal and as the `blood and fuel` of future projects because it protects the human side, rights, responsibilities, Founder, employees, and end users.

**Meaning:** execution systems may vary by project; the Lighthouse human philosophy remains the inherited highest-purpose layer.

---

## P20-F02 — Architect is execution-oriented

**Class:** FOUNDER DIRECT — concept

The Founder distinguishes execution architecture from the Lighthouse Constitution and emphasizes:

- productivity;
- quality;
- accountability;
- full-spectrum end-user experience observation.

This is useful organizational-learning lineage, not Smart Business Product Truth.

---

## P20-F03 — Do not delay; do not rush

**Class:** FOUNDER DIRECT / LIGHTHOUSE-ALIGNED EXECUTION PRINCIPLE

The Founder explicitly connects accountability to the discipline:

`do not delay; do not rush`.

The intended balance is neither endless planning nor reckless speed.

---

## P20-F04 — Context quality requires planned handover

**Class:** FOUNDER DIRECT — concept

The Founder identifies that long-running specialist chats can become bloated and overloaded and proposes handover between rooms — including Room 1 → Room 2 within the same department — to preserve quality.

The durable principle is:

**continuity should be preserved without forcing one conversation to carry unlimited context.**

---

## P20-F05 — Founder formally authorizes Phase 1 execution

**Class:** FOUNDER DIRECT — decision

The Founder explicitly authorizes Phase 1 execution and asks Mission Control to determine:

- first objective;
- responsible specialist room;
- deliverables;
- completion criteria;
- evidence;
- recommended handover.

This is both historical chronology evidence and an operating principle: the Founder owns direction while Mission Control governs sequencing.

# 3. Assistant synthesis candidates worth preserving

## P20-A01 — Quality comes from governed workflow

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE

> Quality does not come from a smarter AI. Quality comes from a governed workflow.

This is one of the strongest organizational-learning formulations in the final planning part.

It should be considered for future Lighthouse organizational-principles review, not treated as current constitutional text automatically.

---

## P20-A02 — Governance should evolve from operational experience

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE

> Governance should evolve from operational experience, not perpetual refinement.

This captures the repeated Part 20 stopping rule: once governance is sufficient, return to execution and let later improvements emerge from actual work.

Current canonical governance later echoes this principle by explicitly avoiding documentation work that slows Phase 1 without proportional value.

---

## P20-A03 — Mission Control governs; specialists execute

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE WITH STRONG CURRENT ECHO

The planning room repeatedly reinforces:

> Mission Control governs. Specialists execute.

Current Source 17/18 later formalizes stage ownership, Mission Control authority, specialist boundaries, and no-self-approval.

---

## P20-A04 — Controlled Handover

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE

The planning formulation becomes:

`Entry Criteria → Responsibilities → Completion Criteria → Handover Package → Exit`.

This is a strong precursor to current repository-first handover rules.

---

## P20-A05 — Every project should leave Lighthouse stronger

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE

> Every Lighthouse project should leave Lighthouse stronger than it found it.

The surrounding planning logic explains that projects should create both:

- external value; and
- internal capability.

This continues the capability-memory and organizational-learning direction first developed strongly in Part 15.

---

## P20-A06 — Architect does not copy project files; it extracts universal capability

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE

This distinction appears while deciding whether the Smart Business Mission Control activation template should be copied directly into Lighthouse Architect.

The planning answer is effectively:

**prove project-specific practice through use → extract reusable capability → promote deliberately.**

This is highly relevant to the future Organizational Learning Engine.

---

## P20-A07 — Founder governs rather than manually manages every step

**Class:** ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS / HISTORICAL CANDIDATE

The planning room describes the successful Mission Control transition as:

> Founder governs the project; Mission Control manages the flow; specialists own execution.

This should not be interpreted as removal of Founder authority. The Founder remains final decision owner.
