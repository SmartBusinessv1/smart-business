## 2. Founding Archive Becomes Historical Memory

Part 13 begins by deciding how the original long planning thread should behave after it is moved into the Smart Business Project.

The recommended use is historical rather than daily execution:

- preserve why decisions happened;
- preserve rejected ideas and corrections;
- preserve product evolution;
- prevent old discussion from competing with reviewed Project Sources;
- start current work in fresh specialist rooms.

The archive-freeze rule is explicit in substance:

> `This chat is now frozen as Smart Business Founding Archive.`

> `Future decisions are governed by Project Sources 00-08.`

> `If any old discussion conflicts with Sources 00-08, the Sources are the authority.`

This is an early project-continuity pattern: history is preserved, but current authority is separated from historical conversation.

## 13. Historical Lessons

### 13.1 Source absorption was treated as a real gate

The Founder did not merely create rooms and assume understanding. Each room had to summarize what it learned and prove its boundary before the next room was activated.

### 13.2 Historical memory and current authority were deliberately separated

The Founding Archive preserved the old reasoning while Sources `00–08` governed current work.

### 13.3 Department / Lab separation was an early containment mechanism

Decision space and troubleshooting space were intentionally separated to reduce noise and accidental authority drift.

### 13.4 The operating system grew from actual founder friction

Infrastructure, Finance and Mission Control emerged because the initial room map left real founder responsibilities uncovered.

### 13.5 Governance grew because execution required it

The later expansion from 9 to 25 files was not the initial state. It was an accumulation of governance, operational and lifecycle needs discovered during execution.

### 13.6 Consolidation was itself a maturity step

The later return from ~25 files to the current 19-authority + README/index package reflects a second lesson: governance should preserve truth without forcing every historical document to remain active.
