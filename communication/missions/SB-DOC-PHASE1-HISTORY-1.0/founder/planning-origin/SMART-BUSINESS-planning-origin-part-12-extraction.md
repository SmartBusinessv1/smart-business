## 3. WhatsApp / OpenAI framework normalization

The planning room reviews the remaining WhatsApp/API prompt set and preserves the useful infrastructure while correcting older assumptions.

Historical decisions/refinements include:

- retain `/api/whatsapp-webhook` as the core inbound endpoint;
- use environment configuration rather than hardcoded provider API versions;
- identify sender role before business processing;
- route Owner, Employee, Supplier, and unknown users differently;
- send unknown users to the Smart Business onboarding route rather than the corporate domain;
- protect compute by avoiding expensive AI/media processing for unregistered or irrelevant requests;
- preserve reminder snooze buttons as a WhatsApp-native operational-memory feature;
- make employee roster/attendance messages factual and merchant-authority-aware rather than Team LIPS acting as employer;
- convert supplier reorder behavior from autonomous AI action into Owner-confirmed or Owner-pre-authorized delegated authority;
- preserve POS/counter signals while replacing theft/fraud accusations with respectful review language;
- preserve support escalation without interrupting normal bookkeeping/business functions.

### Historical boundaries

## 4. AI behaviour and business-boundary maturation

Part 12 expands the AI layer beyond transaction extraction into a clearer business-purpose boundary.

Important historical refinements include:

- intent-first routing rather than assuming every message is a transaction;
- low-confidence interpretation should trigger clarification rather than guessing;
- AI should distinguish transaction, reminder, inventory, supplier order, automation rule, attendance, Ask CFO, support, out-of-scope, and blocked-safety intents;
- unrelated general questions should be redirected rather than turning Smart Business into a general-purpose chatbot;
- unsafe or illegal requests should be refused and treated separately from harmless out-of-scope conversation;
- explicit/unrelated images should be rejected before becoming permanent merchant-file storage;
- business-relevant images/documents may proceed to interpretation and confirmation;
- expensive AI work should happen only after identity, permission, safety, business relevance, and FAQ checks where applicable.

This is a direct historical precursor to the current Business Boundary Guardian and Vision Safety rules in Source 05.

## 5. Support layer and FAQ-first operating model

The planning room treats the FAQ system as both a merchant-trust mechanism and a solo-founder operating-cost control.

The important direction is not the historical exact FAQ count. The durable intent is:

- solve common support questions before escalation;
- give clear, trusted answers without unnecessary compute;
- avoid threatening or ambiguous privacy language;
- preserve Owner authority and employee boundaries;
- escalate unresolved requests while keeping normal Smart Business workflows running.

The Founder explicitly accepts expansion beyond the original FAQ count where that improves customer usefulness.

This later survives in current Source 06 and Source 12 as the FAQ-first support foundation with a 100+ knowledge-base target.
