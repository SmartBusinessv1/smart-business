## 2. Full fifteen-principle historical set

1. **Never use complexity to prove intelligence.** — **FOUNDER DIRECT**
2. **Never let AI remove human dignity.** — **FOUNDER DIRECT**
3. **Discover hidden problems before they become painful.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** of the Founder's direct Principle 3: proactively find problems people may face before they fully recognize them.
4. **Don't assume. Ask.** — **FOUNDER DIRECT core wording**, compressed from the Founder's fuller instruction not to think Team LIPS knows people better than they know themselves.
5. **If we solve, solve exceptionally well.** — **ASSISTANT COMPRESSION** of the Founder's direct Principle 5: Team LIPS cannot solve every problem, but where it can genuinely provide a solution, it should provide the best it can.
6. **Earn retention. Never force retention.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's visible-cancellation and value-retention decision.
7. **Remove friction, not human capability.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the discussion on making life easier without creating dependency or removing human judgement.
8. **Respect the problem more than our solution.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's product-rejection approach: understand why, improve/reposition/bundle where useful, and withdraw when genuine value is absent.
9. **Debate internally with confidence. Serve externally with humility.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's openness to internal challenge while preserving respect for users and their lived experience.
10. **We don't own people's lives; we share a mission with them.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's employee-dignity and no-pressure philosophy.
11. **Measure success by clarity created, not attention captured.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the anti-addiction, anti-dependency, clarity-first philosophy.
12. **Respect users deeply, but never lose self-respect serving them.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's preference for a respectful small merchant over a high-paying customer who damages the team or mission.
13. **Earn through value, not extraction.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's economic principle: `We don't want money they own. We want what we earn by providing value.`
14. **Build for impact, not applause — but honor the humans behind the impact.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's recognition answer: broad fame is not required, but the people who shared or know the Founder's journey should be able to know what was built and who stood behind it.
15. **Understand the human before building for the user.** — **ASSISTANT SYNTHESIS** from the Founder's desired legacy description: `our founder understands people well`, and the recurring pattern of looking behind transactions, subscriptions, employees, students, feedback, and failures to the human reality underneath.

## 3. Provenance safeguard

The future Lighthouse / Team LIPS consolidation must **not** present all fifteen as direct Founder quotations.

The strongest provenance distinction is:

- Principles **1–2** are exact direct Founder formulations.
- Principle **4** preserves direct Founder core wording but in shortened form.
- Principles **3 and 5** are assistant compressions of direct Founder principles.
- Principles **6–15** are planning-co-founder syntheses derived from explicit Founder answers and decisions.

All fifteen are worth preserving because together they show how the planning conversation translated Founder judgement into a reusable decision language. Promotion into current governance, culture doctrine, website copy, hiring principles, or public brand language requires later Founder review under the appropriate mission.

## 4. Later canonical echoes

Several of these historical principles later appear in active Smart Business governance in more formal language, including:

- simplicity over technological display;
- `AI Assistant, Not AI Judge`;
- clarification instead of guessing;
- Build Now / Build Later / Add-on / Separate Product / Reject discipline;
- human decision ownership;
- non-extractive pricing and trust stewardship;
- merchant data ownership;
- culture that combines care with accountability;
- products and technology may change while principles remain.

These echoes strengthen historical continuity but do not retroactively convert assistant-created wording into Founder-authored constitutional text.
