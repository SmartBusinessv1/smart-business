## 1. Why this briefing matters

The briefing is small compared with the full NotebookLM corpus, but it independently confirms that NotebookLM contains substantial Smart Business feature knowledge rather than only a high-level company summary.

The briefing presents Smart Business as an AI-driven business companion for Kerala merchants that turns voice, text, paper, receipts, documents and WhatsApp interactions into structured searchable business memory while preserving the principle that AI assists and humans decide.

It also confirms a broad product family spanning financial memory, document intelligence, daily intelligence, stock, suppliers, POS, HR, compliance, voice, orders/delivery, support and role-based access.

Because this is a briefing, it must be treated as a map to deeper extraction, not as sufficient implementation specification by itself.

## 3. Confirmed NotebookLM briefing detail worth preserving

### Human Language Layer

The briefing says language choice applies to six user roles:

- Owner
- Manager
- Employee
- Delivery Staff
- Supplier
- Customer

Supported languages:

- English
- Malayalam
- Manglish

The deeper extraction must determine exact onboarding, switching, persistence, channel and voice behaviour.

### Ledger / Business Memory

The briefing characterizes the Ledger family as an **AI Business Memory and Daily Clarity System** supporting natural voice/text entries, documents, credit awareness, payment verification, Ask CFO, reminders and daily briefings.

This supports creation of a dedicated Ledger/Business Memory feature record, but the briefing does not yet contain enough detail for a complete implementation-ready contract.

### Daily Intelligence Rhythm

Three scheduled touchpoints are explicitly present:

- 7:00 AM — Morning Business Briefing
- 10:30 AM — Business Pulse Check
- 10:00 PM — Night Closing Intelligence

The briefing explains the purpose as keeping the owner informed without constant notification overload.

The deeper extraction must recover the exact contents, Ledger vs Manager differences, exception rules, timezone handling and cross-feature inputs.

### Receipt Intelligence / Universal Documents

The briefing explicitly connects:

- receipt/paper bill/photo input;
- searchable Receipt Cabinet;
- PDF/Excel/CSV/photo/handwritten input;
- photo-based roster updates;
- handwritten order processing.

The deeper extraction must recover confirmation, duplicate handling, failure behaviour, metadata/storage, correction and export behaviour.

### Smart Credit Awareness

The briefing directly supports:

- customer balance visibility;
- credit warnings / risk awareness;
- Owner retains decision authority.

Further extraction is required for repayment handling, limits, reminders, history, employee/customer visibility and exact relationship with Ledger.

### Stock / Supplier / Reorder

The briefing explicitly describes Stock Intelligence as tracking:

Reorder Intelligence works with Stock Intelligence and Supplier Management to identify restocking needs and support timely purchasing.

Supplier Management interacts with reorder, reminders and multilingual communication.

A future feature set should avoid treating these as isolated systems; deeper extraction must recover the cross-feature workflow and authority boundaries.
