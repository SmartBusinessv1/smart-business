# NotebookLM Founder-Origin Evidence

This folder stores Founder-origin Smart Business evidence recovered from the preserved NotebookLM corpus through structured question-and-answer extraction.

## Purpose

NotebookLM contains early Smart Business product thinking that may be richer than later compressed source summaries.

The extraction method is deliberately question-driven because bulk copy/paste of NotebookLM is impractical.

The Founder has now also recovered the earlier **Ground Zero Smart Business ideation chat**. From this point, the structured recovery uses NotebookLM and Ground Zero as two separate evidence streams so original Founder intent can be compared against later source-era evolution without silently blending them.

## Current files

- `NotebookLM_Extraction_Question_Bank_v1.md` — structured 100-question extraction bank grouped into seven recommended upload batches.
- `NotebookLM_Extraction_Batch_Instructions.md` — reusable instruction for obtaining detailed, provenance-aware NotebookLM answers.
- `NotebookLM_Briefing_Seed_Extraction_01.md` — first seed extraction from the Founder-uploaded NotebookLM briefing.
- `ground-zero-reconciliation/README.md` — temporary seven-section continuity/reconciliation protocol for paired NotebookLM + Ground Zero recovery.
- `ground-zero-reconciliation/Section_*_of_7_*.md` — temporary section-by-section extraction records. These protect continuity until the final Smart Business Feature Definition Library is reconciled and verified.

## Evidence rule

NotebookLM material is historical Founder-origin product evidence.

Ground Zero is earlier Founder-origin product evidence and may contain stronger original product ambition, older technical assumptions, or behaviour that later Founder decisions deliberately corrected.

Both evidence streams can deepen feature definitions and reveal omitted Founder intent, but neither automatically revives obsolete implementation assumptions or supersedes current canonical Product Truth.

Where NotebookLM, Ground Zero, planning history, and current Product Truth differ:

1. preserve each version;
2. preserve chronology;
3. identify the strongest current Founder-supported state;
4. escalate unresolved conflicts for Founder/Mission Control reconciliation;
5. never silently demote an approved feature because an old assistant called it MVP, later, future, post-pilot, or add-on;
6. never silently revive unsafe or superseded behaviour merely because it appeared in Ground Zero.

## Feature-library relationship

Recovered product detail should progressively deepen:

`docs/phase-1-mission-blueprint/smart-business-features/`

A feature is ready for Blueprint/EIS use only when the durable feature record captures complete behaviour, roles, permissions, workflows, exceptions, shared foundations, failure behaviour and acceptance expectations without requiring a builder to reconstruct old conversations.

## Temporary-section lifecycle

The paired extraction sections under `ground-zero-reconciliation/` are intentionally temporary continuity artifacts.

They must remain available until:

- all seven sections are complete;
- the full feature inventory has been reconciled;
- every confirmed feature has a durable final feature definition or explicit destination;
- unresolved conflicts have been settled or explicitly recorded;
- the final feature library has passed repository verification;
- Mission Control has recorded successful completion of Founder-origin feature recovery.

Only then may Mission Control authorize deletion/archive of the temporary section files. Git history remains the provenance record.
