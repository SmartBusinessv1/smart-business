# 6. Founder Room later-era Mission Control succession evidence

The majority of the later Founder Room transcript is not original Product Mission execution. It documents the deliberate transfer of Mission Control capability across successor rooms.

This material is highly relevant to the **Lessons Learned Register** and **Capability Evolution Register**, but should not be misread as direct evidence of early Product Mission identities.

## 6.1 Four-layer state model

**Classification:** `CONFIRMED HISTORICAL OPERATING LEARNING`

The succession/hydration instructions explicitly separate:

- Canonical Governance — what rules govern;
- Institutional Memory — what judgement Mission Control learned;
- Historical Communication — how the project arrived here;
- Current Operational Evidence — where the project is now.

This is a major maturity step.

### Lesson

A project can have multiple kinds of truth simultaneously:

- authority truth;
- historical truth;
- current-state truth;
- implementation/runtime truth.

One layer must not silently replace another.

## 6.2 Mission Memory can be stale

**Classification:** `CONFIRMED HISTORICAL OPERATING LEARNING`

The Founder Room later records a concrete example:

- `mission-control/mission_memory.md` claimed `Active Mission: None`;

The conclusion was not to discard Mission Memory, but to treat it as a current-state claim requiring reconciliation.

### Lesson

> A file named “memory” is not authority merely because it is called memory.

Current-state artifacts must be reconciled against stronger live evidence.

## 6.3 Builder project, preview, production deployment, and production domain are separate states

It separates:

- canonical repository;
- derivative builder repository;
- Lovable implementation workspace;
- preview;
- legacy Lovable workspace;
- production deployment;
- production domain;
- production Supabase.

# 9. Lessons learned extracted from this batch

## LL-FR-01 — Verify control before infrastructure mutation

**Happened:** Phase 1.1 required proof of domain ownership and DNS access before changes.

**Learned:** Possession, visibility, and mutation authority should be proven before changing external infrastructure.

**Evolution:** Later generalized into read-before-write, verify-before-acting, and effective-state verification.

**Scope:** Smart Business governance candidate / organization-wide capability.

---

## LL-FR-02 — Founder reference artifacts can preserve product clarity without becoming authority

**Happened:** The Founder created concise printable product and roadmap references from active sources.

**Learned:** Human operating clarity benefits from compact reference artifacts, but derivative documents must remain subordinate to canonical sources.

**Scope:** Organization-wide documentation capability.

---

## LL-FR-03 — Roadmap numbering and Product Mission numbering must be separated

**Happened:** Founder roadmap artifacts preserve roadmap phases/milestones, while later historical evidence shows executed Product Mission numbering can differ.

**Learned:** Similar numeric labels are not sufficient evidence of identity.

**Changed practice:** Historical reconstruction requires direct mission-label evidence before mapping roadmap milestones to `SB-P-*` missions.

**Scope:** Smart Business historical/governance discipline.

---

## LL-FR-04 — Platform account identity requires organizational design

**Happened:** Before SB-P1.3, the Founder explicitly considered whether Lovable, Supabase, Meta, R2, ElevenLabs and related platforms should use Team LIPS business identity or personal email.

**Learned:** Account ownership, login identity, mailbox, billing identity, and operational authority must not be conflated.

**Scope:** Team LIPS platform-operations capability.

---

## LL-FR-05 — Current-state artifacts must be reconciled, not trusted by label

**Happened:** Later Mission Memory could say no active mission while stronger live repository evidence showed ongoing SB-P-1.11 work.

**Learned:** Memory/current-state records can drift.

**Changed practice:** Reconcile against current repository, communication, mission artifacts, and platform evidence.

**Scope:** Mission Control institutional capability.

---

## LL-FR-06 — Production truth is multi-layered

**Happened:** Successor hydration explicitly separated builder publication, preview, repository state, production deployment/domain, and backend identity.

**Learned:** A single platform status cannot establish overall production truth.

**Scope:** Team LIPS deployment/recovery capability.

---

## LL-FR-07 — Hydration must transfer judgement, history, and current state separately

**Happened:** Mission Control succession was split into governance, institutional memory, historical communication, current-state reconciliation, and calibration.

**Learned:** A successor should inherit not just documents, but the ability to distinguish what each document can legitimately prove.

**Scope:** Organization-wide AI operating-system capability.
