## 1. Evidence method

Historical claims use the mission evidence labels:

- `CONFIRMED`
- `FOUNDER-CLARIFIED — STRONGLY CORROBORATED`
- `STRONGLY SUPPORTED`
- `CHAT-ONLY HISTORICAL`
