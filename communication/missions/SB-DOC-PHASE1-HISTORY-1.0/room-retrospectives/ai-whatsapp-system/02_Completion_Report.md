## 3. Major corrections and superseded assumptions surfaced

The retrospective records these major corrections:

- WhatsApp-first does **not** mean WhatsApp owns Smart Business architecture.
- Current architecture is conversation-first and channel-independent.
- `SB-P-1.13` Native Conversation & AI Intelligence and `SB-P-1.20` WhatsApp Channel Integration must remain separate.
- Channel adapters must not duplicate Business Memory, permissions, AI policy, confirmation logic or domain rules.
- A phone number is an identity/routing signal, not permission.
- Model confidence is not authority.
- Consequential confirmation must bind to the exact actor, business, action, target, payload and reviewed state.
- Permission must be revalidated at execution time.
- Cross-user confirmation authority cannot be implied.
- Provider-event deduplication and business-command idempotency are separate concerns.
- Pre-command parsing/media/model failures are not `UNKNOWN_OUTCOME`; that state applies only when an authoritative command may have committed but the result is uncertain.
- WhatsApp delivery state is separate from business-action state.
- Receipt-specific OCR/vision logic must not be silently generalized into an independent channel-owned media architecture.
- OpenAI interprets/reasons; Smart Business permissions, deterministic services and human authority control what may happen.
- Calibration, mature contracts, provider setup and architecture review are not implementation-completion evidence.
