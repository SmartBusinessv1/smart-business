# 1. Lessons Learned

## 1.1 Provenance classes must stay separate

`CORRECTION / LESSON`

Research & Intelligence learned that Smart Business evidence cannot be flattened into one class. The following sources answer different questions and must remain distinguishable:

- **Direct Founder wording** — strongest evidence of what the Founder actually said at that point in time.
- **Founder-origin paraphrase** — useful evidence of intent when traceable, but weaker than direct wording.
- **NotebookLM synthesis/extraction** — useful for structured recovery and cross-source organization, but generated synthesis must be verified against its underlying evidence.
- **Ground Zero recovery** — historically valuable for earliest-known Founder intent, merchant problems, ambition and reasoning; it may also contain obsolete technical assumptions or more aggressive historical mechanisms.
- **Historical room/chat evidence** — can establish chronology, prior decisions, prior misunderstandings and implementation history, but does not automatically control current truth.
- **Merged repository evidence** — strongest durable evidence of current approved documents, accepted mission outputs and canonical repository state.
- **Runtime evidence** — required to establish what is actually deployed or operational; repository state alone cannot prove runtime state.
- **Current Product Truth** — controls current approved product behaviour, permissions, packaging and boundaries.
- **Later assistant interpretation** — useful only as analysis unless independently supported; it is not Founder wording or Product Truth.
- **Unresolved inference** — must remain explicitly unresolved.

Durable rule:

> Historical evidence may explain **why** Smart Business reached its current shape, but it does not automatically control **what is current**.

## 1.2 Evidence type determines what may be claimed

`CAPABILITY PROVEN`

Research & Intelligence developed a practical evidence hierarchy:

| Question | Strongest evidence class |
|---|---|
| What happened historically? | dated primary room/chat/repository evidence, preserved historical artifacts |
| What is current authority? | Founder decisions + current canonical governance/Product Truth |
| What is implemented in repository? | current merged repository inspection |
| What is deployed/runtime truth? | runtime verification, platform evidence, logs, production checks |
| What did the Founder intend? | direct Founder wording, then carefully traced Founder-origin evidence |
| What is architectural direction? | current Product Truth + approved contracts/build plan/EIS/governance |

This prevented historical richness from being mistaken for current authority and prevented current source compression from erasing useful historical reasoning.

## 1.3 Ground Zero was valuable because it recovered the problem and ambition behind later compressed labels

`CAPABILITY PROVEN`

Ground Zero helped recover:

- original merchant problems;
- original Founder intent;
- workflow ambition and UX expectations;
- early examples and reasoning;
- cross-feature relationships;
- early automation assumptions;
- product capabilities that later summaries compressed into short labels.

Its value was greatest where later source-era documents preserved a feature name but lost why it existed or how deeply it was meant to work.

Its limitation is equally important: Ground Zero contains historical implementation assumptions and behaviours that were later corrected. It cannot override current Product Truth simply because it is earlier or more detailed.

## 1.4 NotebookLM was effective for structured extraction, not authority creation

`CAPABILITY PROVEN`

NotebookLM helped organize a large Founder-origin corpus through question-driven extraction. It was especially useful for:

- structured retrieval across broad source material;
- surfacing omitted details;
- collecting examples, workflows, roles and historical packaging labels;
- comparing multiple source-era formulations;
- identifying contradictions that required reconciliation.

The 100-question bank was deliberately designed to ask for source-supported detail, conflicts, examples and residual omissions instead of accepting high-level summaries.

NotebookLM output itself is not Product Truth. Generated synthesis had to be treated as evidence requiring verification and reconciliation.

## 1.5 Questions 1–100 were an anti-compression device, not merely a questionnaire

`CORRECTION / LESSON`

The value of Questions 1–100 was not simply producing 100 answers. The framework forced recovery across seven controlled sections and exposed:

- omitted features;
- compressed feature depth;
- misunderstood add-ons;
- cross-feature dependencies;
- historical mechanisms masquerading as current requirements;
- contradictory pricing/timing/permission assumptions;
- Founder corrections to earlier assistant advice;
- unresolved decisions that must remain unresolved.

The sectioned method also reduced shallow extraction and made missing evidence visible instead of encouraging invention.

## 1.6 Missing evidence must remain missing

`MISTAKE / FAILURE MODE`

A partial export, room archive, search result or assistant summary must never be treated as complete project history.

Research learned to preserve these distinctions:

- absence of evidence is not proof of absence;
- a search-result snippet is not the same as source text;
- similar wording in two documents does not prove identical provenance;
- an assistant summary cannot be quoted as direct Founder wording;
- a provider or technical mechanism mentioned historically does not become Product Truth unless reaffirmed;
- an attractive historical sentence cannot outrank stronger current authority.

## 1.7 Preserve capability; reject obsolete mechanism

`CORRECTION / LESSON`

A durable research model emerged:

1. identify the **merchant problem**;
2. identify the **Founder-intended capability**;
3. identify the **historical mechanism**;
4. identify the **later correction**;
5. map to the **mature feature contract/current Product Truth**.

Recovered material was then classified as:

- `PRESERVE — STILL CURRENT`
- `PRESERVE + EVOLVE`
- `HISTORICAL IMPLEMENTATION DETAIL`
- `SUPERSEDED / REJECTED BEHAVIOUR`
- `UNRESOLVED FOUNDER DECISION`

This avoids two opposite errors:

- deleting a valuable Founder-created capability because its first mechanism was weak;
- reviving obsolete, unsafe or over-rigid behaviour because it appeared in early history.

The durable rule is:

> Preserve the approved feature. Preserve the Founder correction. Evolve the mechanism. Reject only superseded behaviour.

## 1.8 Current Product Truth must not be silently corrected by historical research

`CORRECTION / LESSON`

The Current-Source Reconciliation Ledger established an important boundary: historical recovery may expose stale, compressed or ambiguous source wording, but Research & Intelligence does not amend constitutional Product Truth.

When historical recovery reveals a likely clarification candidate:

- document the evidence;
- classify the conflict;
- identify whether it is historical-only, clarification-worthy or unresolved;
- route it to Mission Control/Founder;
- do not edit authority silently.

## 1.9 External research is useful only for external facts

`CAPABILITY PROVEN`

External research is appropriate for:

- legal/regulatory understanding;
- provider capabilities;
- technical standards;
- industry practice;
- merchant-market terminology;
- implementation alternatives.

It is not appropriate for deciding:

- Founder intent;
- current Smart Business Product Truth;
- internal mission authority;
- whether a historical feature was approved;
- whether an implementation exists in Smart Business.

Those require internal evidence.

The Kerala Market Catalog Glossary demonstrated this boundary well: official Kerala/India sources informed vocabulary and UX recommendations, while locked Smart Business decisions continued to control category ownership, custom entry and no-conversion behaviour.

## 1.10 Research should surface contradictions; humans resolve authority

`CURRENT — STILL VALID`

Research & Intelligence became most useful when it stopped trying to be the final product authority.

The correct flow became:

Founder-origin evidence  
→ reconciliation  
→ evidence classification  
→ mature feature/foundation contracts  
→ global completion register  
→ Founder-approved canonical build plan.

Research provides evidence, provenance, contradiction detection and options. Mission Control and the Founder resolve product/governance authority.

# 4. Suggested Tools to Have

Every item below is `RECOMMENDATION — NOT YET ADOPTED`.

## 4.1 Provenance-aware institutional search

Problem solved: semantic search may rank an old historical artifact above current authority.

Recommendation: add a search layer that carries metadata for authority, date, supersession, evidence type and current/historical status.

Risk: bad metadata could create false confidence; human-review and repository authority must remain primary.

## 4.2 Structured decision/evidence registry

Problem solved: Founder decisions, evidence, current authority and unresolved questions can become distributed across many files.

Recommendation: maintain a machine-readable registry linking decisions to direct evidence, current controlling source, superseded variants and unresolved dependencies.

Risk: registry must not become a competing Product Truth authority.

## 4.3 Citation/evidence graph

Problem solved: later researchers may struggle to distinguish direct Founder wording from paraphrase, synthesis and downstream contracts.

Recommendation: represent evidence edges such as `direct-source`, `paraphrases`, `supersedes`, `derived-from`, `contradicts`, `implemented-by`, `verified-at-runtime`.

Risk: graph relations require careful human-reviewed provenance.

## 4.4 Automated contradiction detection

Problem solved: terminology, timing, packaging and authority rules can drift across large source sets.

Recommendation: automated comparison that flags potential contradictions without resolving them.

Risk: similarity or difference detection must not become automated governance judgement.

## 4.5 Source-version comparison

Problem solved: difficult manual review of what changed between historical and current source versions.

Recommendation: a source-aware diff workflow that highlights product-behaviour, permission, packaging and authority changes separately from formatting edits.

Risk: semantic classification remains advisory.

## 4.6 Room-history export/archive tooling

Problem solved: incomplete chat exports create continuity gaps.

Recommendation: standard export/archival workflow with timestamps, room identity, message provenance and attachment references.

Risk: privacy, volume and duplicate-history management.

## 4.7 Structured Founder-decision capture

Problem solved: critical Founder decisions can be buried inside long conversational exchanges.

Recommendation: capture each material decision with exact wording where available, rationale, date, affected Product Truth area and status.

Risk: must preserve nuance and avoid forcing every Founder reflection into a formal decision.

## 4.8 Evidence confidence metadata

Problem solved: researchers may present all evidence with equal certainty.

Recommendation: standard metadata such as `direct`, `traceable paraphrase`, `synthesis`, `inference`, `runtime verified`, `repository verified`, `unresolved`.

Risk: confidence labels assist judgement; they do not replace source reading.

# 5. Suggestions to Improve This Project

## 5.1 Keep current authority and historical evidence physically and semantically distinct

Current governance/Product Truth should remain easy to identify even when historical evidence is preserved permanently.

## 5.2 Add provenance metadata to durable historical-recovery artifacts

Every long-lived recovery file should make clear:

- original evidence stream;
- whether wording is direct or synthesized;
- chronology;
- current/superseded/unresolved status;
- successor artifact where one exists.

## 5.3 Preserve contradiction ledgers instead of silently normalizing history

The Current-Source Reconciliation Ledger is a strong pattern. Future deep-research missions should preserve disagreements until the proper authority resolves them.

## 5.4 Require explicit evidence class in high-risk research conclusions

Claims about Founder intent, Product Truth, implementation or runtime should always state the evidence class supporting them.

## 5.5 Maintain one global product-completion view

Research recovery showed that local mission scope can accidentally hide global product commitments. The global view should remain the anti-omission control.

## 5.6 Keep market research subordinate to product identity

Market evidence should improve vocabulary, UX, prioritization and implementation choices without turning competitor or industry convention into authority over Smart Business.

## 5.7 Preserve “why” alongside “what”

Feature contracts and decision records should retain the merchant problem and Founder rationale where durable, because these help future builders evolve mechanisms safely.

# 6. What Future Rooms Must Know Before Touching This Area

1. GitHub `main` is the operational source of truth; do not execute from an old room memory.
2. During Phase 1, current constitutional Product Truth comes from the approved authority chain; historical recovery is subordinate.
3. NotebookLM and Ground Zero are separate evidence streams. Do not merge them into one unattributed voice.
4. Direct Founder wording, Founder-origin paraphrase, assistant synthesis and inference are not equivalent.
5. Historical implementation detail may explain a design, but it does not automatically bind current engineering.
6. Never infer that a capability was rejected merely because an old assistant called it “future,” “later,” “MVP,” “post-pilot” or “add-on.”
7. Never infer that an old mechanism is current merely because it appears in rich Founder-origin history.
8. Check the Final Feature Reconciliation Register, Current Source Reconciliation Ledger, mature 25-contract library, global completion view and canonical build plan before drawing current-product conclusions.
9. Repository truth and runtime truth are different. Use runtime evidence for deployment claims.
10. Research can recommend. Mission Control/Founder decide Product Truth and governance.
11. Missing evidence must stay missing; mark uncertainty rather than filling it with model knowledge.
12. External web research cannot determine internal Smart Business authority or implementation state.

# 10. Open Questions / Residual Risks

## 10.1 Incomplete historical evidence remains possible

`UNRESOLVED / NEEDS FOUNDER OR MISSION CONTROL DECISION`

Some early room/chat history may remain incomplete or available only through partial exports. Future researchers must avoid presenting the reconstructed corpus as mathematically complete when evidence gaps remain.

## 10.2 Direct-Founder wording versus later paraphrase confidence

`UNRESOLVED / NEEDS FOUNDER OR MISSION CONTROL DECISION`

Some durable conclusions rely on traceable Founder-origin paraphrase rather than exact direct wording. Provenance labels should remain visible where wording precision matters.

## 10.3 Historical-source ranking risk

`UNRESOLVED / NEEDS FOUNDER OR MISSION CONTROL DECISION`

Future semantic search may surface old rich history above current authority. This is a retrieval risk, not a Product Truth ambiguity, and supports the recommendation for authority-aware search metadata.

## 10.4 Automated provenance/citation infrastructure is still limited

`RECOMMENDATION — NOT YET ADOPTED`

The project has strong manual provenance discipline but no complete automated evidence graph or contradiction engine.

## 10.5 Current source clarification candidates remain governance matters

`UNRESOLVED / NEEDS FOUNDER OR MISSION CONTROL DECISION`

The Current-Source Reconciliation Ledger records clarification candidates around the first-class Conversation Workspace and its complete multimodal surface. Research must preserve those as candidates, not amendments.

## 10.6 Current unresolved Founder decisions touched by historical research

Do not resolve through research:

- free-trial policy;
- exact Voice Plus price;
- exact Staff/HR price;
- exact Smart Stock Assistant price;
- exact Smart Order & Delivery price;
- exact long-term retention/deletion duration after cancellation/non-payment;
- whether employee KYC/national-ID data is a committed requirement and its legal/privacy basis;
- broader wholesaler/marketplace expansion;
- future underwriting/lending/third-party financial ecosystem direction.

## 10.7 Research-to-authority drift remains a permanent risk

`CURRENT — STILL VALID`

The more complete a research artifact becomes, the easier it is for a future AI to mistake it for authority. Every deep-research deliverable should therefore preserve its authority boundary, evidence classes and escalation route.
