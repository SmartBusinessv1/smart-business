## 1. Founder direction

The Founder requires that, until Stage 4B background automation is implemented and accepted, Mission Control must not rely on memory alone to initiate organizational learning after mission closure.

Standing operational rule:

> No future mission is fully closed for communication-closeout purposes until Mission Control has either initiated an OLE learning handoff from the authoritative closure or explicitly recorded `no reusable learning` with supporting evidence.

This rule applies to future Smart Business missions that reach formal closure.

---

## 2. Operational effect

While Stage 4B is absent, every mission closure must include one of:

### A. OLE learning handoff

Mission Control explicitly initiates the accepted OLE process against the closed mission's authoritative evidence so that reusable learning can be harvested, validated, reviewed and surfaced to future missions.

### B. Explicit no-reusable-learning disposition

Mission Control records that the mission produced no material reusable organizational learning and preserves enough evidence to support that conclusion.

A mission may proceed to communication archive / final closeout only after one of these states is durably recorded.

---

## 3. Scope and authority boundary

This is a Mission Control operating rule for closure discipline.

It does not:

- authorize Stage 4B implementation;
- authorize background automation;
- authorize provider/model integration;
- authorize credentials or scheduler/background workers;
- authorize trusted publisher / PR creation automation;
- authorize automatic promotion;
- authorize automatic merge;
- authorize governance or Product Truth mutation;
- authorize production/customer mutation;
- activate `SB-P-1.12`.

The rule remains manual until Stage 4B is separately implemented, independently verified and accepted.
