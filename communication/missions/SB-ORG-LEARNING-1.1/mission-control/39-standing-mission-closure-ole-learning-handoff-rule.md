# SMART BUSINESS MISSION CONTROL

# SB-ORG-LEARNING-1.1 — Standing Mission Closure Learning-Handoff Rule

**Date:** 2026-09-18

**Authority:** Smart Business Mission Control under Founder direction

**Applies to:** future Smart Business missions and formal Mission Control closeouts

**Status:** `ACTIVE — STANDING OPERATIONAL RULE`

**Stage 4B tracking issue:** `#590 — OLE Stage 4B — Background Automation & Candidate Learning Runtime`

---

## 1. Purpose

Until the deferred Stage 4B background automation is implemented and formally accepted, Smart Business must not rely on memory or informal habit to initiate Organizational Learning Engine processing.

The accepted OLE v1 foundation can safely structure, validate, reconcile and reuse organizational learning, but it does not yet automatically notice every authoritative mission closure and initiate the full candidate-learning flow in the background.

Therefore Mission Control establishes the following standing closure rule.

---

## 2. Standing rule

A future Smart Business mission must not be considered fully closed by Mission Control until one of the following is durably recorded:

### A. OLE learning handoff initiated

The mission closeout explicitly hands the authoritative closure into the OLE learning lifecycle for:

- closure-envelope / authoritative-closure reconciliation;
- approved evidence harvesting;
- candidate lesson / risk extraction;
- claim-level provenance validation;
- contradiction / limitation retention;
- human review / promotion where warranted;
- reusable context refresh where warranted.

or:

### B. No material reusable learning

Mission Control explicitly records:

`NO MATERIAL REUSABLE LEARNING`

with a short evidence-based reason.

This is not permission to skip OLE silently.

It is an explicit closure outcome.

---

## 3. Closure sequence until Stage 4B exists

The expected manual operating sequence is:

```text
mission implementation complete
        ↓
independent / runtime verification complete as applicable
        ↓
Mission Control acceptance
        ↓
Founder/human merge when required
        ↓
post-merge verification
        ↓
authoritative mission closure
        ↓
OLE learning handoff
        ↓
candidate learning / risks / follow-ups prepared
        ↓
human review / promotion where warranted
        ↓
reusable context refreshed
        ↓
communication closeout complete
```

If the OLE review concludes there is no material reusable learning, record that explicitly and proceed to closeout.

---

## 4. Authority boundary

This standing rule changes the **closure procedure**, not the authority hierarchy.

OLE remains:

- context and organizational memory, not governance;
- a candidate-learning system, not an approval authority;
- unable to activate Product Missions;
- unable to alter Product Truth;
- unable to alter governance;
- unable to self-promote;
- unable to self-merge;
- unable to deploy or mutate production/customer state.

Mission Control/human authority remains responsible for review and promotion.

---

## 5. Relationship to Stage 4B

GitHub issue #590 tracks the deferred Stage 4B capability whose purpose is to remove this manual initiation dependency.

Stage 4B should eventually replace:

`human remembers to initiate OLE`

with:

`authoritative mission closure automatically creates a bounded OLE candidate-learning run`

while retaining human review/promotion and all existing authority boundaries.

Until Stage 4B is implemented, independently verified and formally accepted, this standing manual closure rule remains active.

---

## 6. Mission-close checklist addition

For every future mission, Mission Control closeout must answer:

1. Has authoritative closure been established?
2. Has OLE learning been initiated against that closure?
3. If not, is `NO MATERIAL REUSABLE LEARNING` explicitly recorded with reason?
4. Are unresolved risks/follow-ups preserved?
5. Has reusable context been refreshed where applicable?

A missing answer to both item 2 and item 3 means:

`MISSION CLOSEOUT INCOMPLETE — OLE LEARNING HANDOFF REQUIRED`
