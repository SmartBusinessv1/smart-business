# SMART BUSINESS — FOUNDER DECISION RECORD 06

## SB-P-1.12 — Phase One Legal Validation Deferral and Research Substitution

**Status:** DRAFT — INEFFECTIVE UNTIL FOUNDER/AUTHORIZED-HUMAN MERGE AND MISSION CONTROL POST-MERGE VERIFICATION  
**Founder:** Riyas PK  
**Mission:** SB-P-1.12 — Authority, Identity & Product Surface Foundation  
**Date:** 2026-09-28

## 1. Founder decision

For the remainder of Phase One Smart Business product-building work through completion of **SB-P-1.20**, external human legal/privacy/retention validation shall **not be treated as a blocker to continuing Phase One build progress**.

Instead, the unresolved legal/privacy/retention questions identified in MC-109 and MC-111 shall, for Phase One build purposes, be researched in depth by the **Research & Intelligence** room using authoritative Indian legal and regulatory sources.

That research may be used by Mission Control as **provisional Phase One legal/compliance evidence** for source-accounting and product-build sequencing.

## 2. What this decision changes

For Phase One build governance only:

- the mandatory immediate external-professional validation gate established by MC-111 is **deferred**;
- Research & Intelligence is authorized to perform deep legal research on the MC-111 LV-1 through LV-12 questions;
- Mission Control may reconcile that research and, if sufficiently grounded and internally consistent, use it to continue Phase One source-accounting and build work;
- the absence of an external legal opinion shall not, by itself, block completion of SB-P-1.12 or subsequent Phase One SB-P missions through SB-P-1.20.

## 3. What this decision does not change

This is **not** a finding that professional legal review is unnecessary.

It does not:

- convert AI/web research into legal advice;
- certify statutory compliance;
- waive any applicable law;
- authorize knowingly unlawful retention or deletion;
- approve a final legal retention schedule;
- finalize Privacy Policy or Terms & Conditions;
- authorize publication of legal statements that have not been professionally validated;
- remove ESC-1 or any security/integrity blocker;
- authorize unsafe deletion implementation.

## 4. Deferred professional-validation milestone

After completion of the full Phase One SB-P build through **SB-P-1.20**, the Founder intends to engage a competent legal professional for detailed preparation/review of:

- Smart Business Privacy Policy;
- Terms & Conditions / Terms of Service;
- applicable retention/privacy/compliance positions;
- any related merchant/customer legal documentation.

At that stage, the Phase One legal research and any implementation assumptions derived from it must be validated.

If professional review identifies required changes, those changes shall be routed as a post-Phase-One legal/compliance correction mission before final legal-policy publication and any production reliance that requires those legal conclusions.

## 5. Phase One operating rule

Mission Control shall distinguish clearly between:

**Provisional research-backed Phase One assumption**  
and  
**Professionally validated legal requirement**.

Where Research & Intelligence finds material uncertainty, conflicting authority, merchant-class dependence or unresolved applicability, Mission Control must preserve that uncertainty explicitly rather than presenting the result as settled law.

## 6. Authority

This Founder decision changes only the **timing and gating treatment** of external professional legal validation for Phase One.

It does not change Founder Record 05 ESC1-6 / ESC1-7 Product Truth or the requirement to preserve lawful record retention, privacy minimization, business history and human authority.
