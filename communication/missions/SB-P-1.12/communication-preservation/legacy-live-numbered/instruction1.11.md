## 2. Evidence tracks

### Track A — Legal / Privacy / Retention

**Questions**

- L-1: Which business, financial, tax, audit, security and operational records must remain after a business or personal identity is deleted, and for what legally or policy-required periods?
- L-2: Which personal data tied to the Smart Business identity must be erased, may be retained, or must be retained, and on what lawful/policy basis?
- L-3: May audit attribution identifiers survive identity deletion, and if so in what minimized/pseudonymized form?

**Required evidence**

A written determination from a competent legal/privacy/retention-policy authority. It must distinguish:
- mandatory retention;
- optional business retention;
- personal-data erasure/minimization;
- audit attribution;
- deletion proof;
- unresolved jurisdiction-specific matters.

**Boundary**

AI specialist rooms may prepare the question set and organize evidence, but they must not manufacture legal conclusions, statutory periods or legal authority. External/public law research may inform the later professional review but is not, by itself, the authoritative completion evidence required by `instruction1.9.md`.

### Track B — Finance & Payments

**Questions**

- C-1: Which unresolved commercial states, if any, should block identity deletion: unpaid dues, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, pending payouts, reconciliation exceptions or other settlement conditions?
- Which states belong to the business/subscription rather than the personal identity?
- What evidence proves commercial settlement is complete?
- What must survive identity deletion for audit/accounting/reconciliation?

**Required evidence**

A written Finance & Payments finding that separates:
1. business/subscription obligations;
2. personal-identity obligations;
3. conditions that genuinely block identity deletion;
4. conditions that should not block deletion;
5. evidence source of truth and owner.

No payment-provider implementation or accounting design is authorized.

### Track C — SB-P-1.19 Lifecycle / Support

**Questions**

- C-2: Does an open support case, complaint, cancellation process, export request, deletion request or reactivation state block personal-identity deletion?
- What constitutes authoritative proof that the final owned business has been permissibly deleted?
- Which merchant-facing lifecycle steps are owned by SB-P-1.19 versus authority/integrity foundations owned by SB-P-1.12?
- Does Contract 18's use of “account/business” explicitly include personal identity deletion? If not, record that ambiguity rather than resolving it by assumption.

**Required evidence**

A written lifecycle/source finding with exact Contract 18 and Build Plan citations, explicit unresolved ambiguity, evidence owner and acceptance dependency.

No SB-P-1.19 Blueprint, FCTM or implementation work is authorized by this instruction.

### Track D — Security & Permissions / Foundation Facts

**Questions**

- F-1: How is “no authorized membership remains” authoritatively established?
- F-2: How is “final owned business deleted” established across ownership state and lifecycle deletion proof?
