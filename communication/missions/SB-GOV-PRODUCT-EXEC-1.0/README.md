## Objective

Reconcile the Product Mission execution system so future `SB-P-1.12` through `SB-P-1.20` missions can execute faster, securely, traceably and scalably without weakening security, human authority, independent verification, runtime evidence, production default-deny controls or repository auditability.

This mission does not change Product Truth.

It optimizes how already-approved Product Truth, feature contracts, build direction, institutional memory, OLE learning, engineering evidence and human decisions move through the Product Mission lifecycle.

---

## Founder-approved target outputs

This mission shall produce exactly these primary governance outcomes:

1. one optimized Source 18 lifecycle;
2. one aligned Product Feature Elaboration / Product Blueprint template;
3. one aligned Implementation & Evidence template;
4. one updated Founder-approved build-plan operational baseline;
5. one communication/migration efficiency rule set.

Supporting index/status changes may be made only where required to activate those outcomes cleanly.

---

## Founder-approved execution principles

The optimized Product Mission model must preserve:

- Founder as final product authority;
- Mission Control orchestration and acceptance;
- no self-approval;
- no self-verification;
- mandatory independent Stage 19 verification for every Product Mission;
- protected-main pull-request workflow;
- human merge authority;
- server/database-side authorization;
- cross-tenant isolation;
- execution-time permission revalidation;
- negative-path testing where relevant;
- environment identity verification;
- production default-deny mutation controls;
- claim-specific evidence;
- runtime verification;
- OLE learning disposition before formal mission closure.

The optimization must remove unnecessary rediscovery, duplicated deterministic verification, provider-specific lifecycle assumptions, and clerical stage/PR coupling.

---

## Locked no-rediscovery rule

Future Product Missions shall not rediscover already-approved Product Truth by default.

Where current approved sources, mature feature contracts, Founder-approved build-plan artifacts and current repository/runtime state already answer a question, the mission shall consume that truth rather than ask the Founder to decide it again.

Founder Discovery becomes a conditional exception gate for genuine unresolved Product Truth, conflicting approved sources, or a new product decision.

---

## Locked institutional-learning intake rule

Founder approved:

> **Every Product Mission intake must consume both current validated OLE learning and the canonical Phase 1 institutional-memory guide until Mission Control verifies that the historical OLE backfill is complete.**

Canonical Phase 1 institutional-memory guide:

`docs/phase-1-mission-blueprint/00_Phase_1_Institutional_Memory_Lessons_Capabilities_and_Operational_Guardrails.md`

Current validated OLE learning source:

`organizational-learning/promotions/**`

This rule exists because pre-OLE lessons were durably reconstructed into canonical institutional memory but have not all been normalized into OLE candidate/promotion records.

Until historical backfill completion is explicitly verified, neither source may substitute for the other.

After backfill completion, Mission Control may separately authorize a simplified intake rule if the evidence demonstrates OLE fully covers the required historical institutional learning.

This mission does not itself perform the full historical OLE backfill unless separately authorized.
