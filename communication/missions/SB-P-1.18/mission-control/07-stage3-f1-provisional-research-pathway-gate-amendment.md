## 2. The amendment

**Condition replaced, for F-1 only.** The `06-…` §5 F-1 "Preserved condition" read: "**Qualified Indian legal/privacy advice is a prerequisite to a binding Founder decision.** No retention number may be proposed or applied before it". From the effective date of this record, that condition is replaced for F-1 by the conditions below. The `06-…` file is not edited and keeps that text as history.

**F1-R conditions.** The Founder may decide F-1 as a **provisional Phase One product-design assumption** under Founder Decision Record 06 only when all of the following hold:

1. **This record is canonical.** It has been human-merged, and Mission Control has verified it on `main`.
2. **Location-specific research is complete and accepted.** A provisional, non-lawyer research report covers the three classes: employee attendance location, delivery-proof location and customer delivery destination. It must:
   - be cited and dated;
   - distinguish processing and access expiry from retention and lawful exceptions;
   - distinguish mandatory obligations from design options and from legal uncertainty;
   - be recorded in the repository with its provenance;
   - be independently reviewed and accepted by Mission Control.
3. **No material legal or privacy blocker is open.** If the research identifies one, or cannot support a value, work stops and the matter is escalated to the Founder. No provisional answer is forced. Route F1-P, qualified advice first, remains available.
4. **Values come only from the research.** No retention number, duration or rule is proposed before condition 2 is met. Afterwards, a value may be presented only as an option supported by that research and selected by the Founder. Retention stays record-class-specific and is never inferred from business-lifecycle windows (C22 §33.4).
5. **The decision record is labelled truthfully.** Each F-1 value is labelled "provisional research-backed Phase One assumption — not professionally validated". The record states that it approves no final legal retention schedule, no compliance clearance and no production legal reliance (Founder Decision Record 06 §3).
6. **Validation and holds stay.**
   - Professional legal and privacy validation stays a deferred requirement under Founder Decision Record 06 §4, before final legal-policy publication and any production reliance on these conclusions.
   - `DLEG` stays on `22-§16-6` and on every row that carries it.
   - Lawful disposal, minimisation and security protections are unchanged.
