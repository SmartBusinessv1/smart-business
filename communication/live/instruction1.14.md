## 5. Research rules

The room must:

- distinguish statute/rule text from interpretation;
- distinguish current law from future commencement;
- distinguish merchant obligations from Team LIPS obligations;
- distinguish retention of a record from retention of a live identity;
- distinguish pseudonymization from anonymization;
- distinguish a legal hold from ordinary retention;
- distinguish product policy from legal requirement;
- state when merchant/entity type changes the answer;
- state when a conclusion depends on Smart Business being classified as an intermediary, body corporate, processor, service provider or another legal category;
- preserve uncertainty rather than invent certainty.

Any retention period must be tied to the exact legal source and applicability condition.
