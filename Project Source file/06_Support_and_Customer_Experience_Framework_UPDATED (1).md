For sensitive merchant topics avoid:

-   normally

-   owner controlled

# 4. Merchant Data Ownership Shield

FAQ: Will GST, Income Tax, government departments, or others get my
business data?

Answer:

Your business data belongs to you.

Smart Business is your private business assistant created to help you
manage your own shop.

Team LIPS and Smart Business are independent technology services.

We are not affiliated with GST, Income Tax, or any government
department.

We are not a reporting platform for any government or private
organization.

We do not sell, share, voluntarily provide, or use your:

-   sales records
-   business figures
-   customer details
-   stock information
-   financial reports

for any third party.

Your data exists inside Smart Business only for providing the services
you subscribed for.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Team LIPS Access Principle

FAQ: Can Team LIPS see my business data?

Answer:

Team LIPS does not view or access your business information.

If technical support requires checking account-specific information, it
will be done only with mutual agreement for that support purpose.

Your business information is not monitored, reviewed, or analyzed by
Team LIPS staff as part of daily operations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Business Model Trust Principle

The merchant is not the product.

The merchant's data is not the product.

Smart Business earns from subscriptions.

Not by selling business intelligence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Employee Access FAQ

FAQ: Can my employees see my business details?

Answer:

No.

Employees added to Smart Business are not given owner access.

Employee access is limited to:

-   adding assigned transactions
-   uploading allowed receipts/documents
-   marking attendance
-   requesting attendance corrections
-   viewing their own attendance records

Employees cannot view:

-   profit
-   sales reports
-   cash position
-   customer credit reports
-   supplier analytics
-   business insights
-   Ask CFO answers

Owner information always stays owner-only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. AI Authority FAQ

FAQ: Will AI control my business?

Answer:

No.

Smart Business follows:

AI Assistant, Not AI Judge.

AI can:

-   organize
-   calculate
-   remind
-   suggest

Business decisions and actions always remain under owner authority.

Smart Business performs actions based only on:

-   owner's direct instruction
-   owner's previously approved rules
