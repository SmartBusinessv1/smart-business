# Mission Control — Founder Narrow Drift Correction Protocol

**Scope:** Founder Room assistance with Mission Control corrections  
**Purpose:** Correct one unsupported assumption without inadvertently redesigning or restarting an otherwise valid Product Mission.  
**Status:** Proposed Founder operational reference, subject to Founder human review and merge. Does not override canonical governance or appoint Founder Room as Mission Control.  
**Repository:** `SmartBusinessv1/smart-business`

## Fundamental rule

**Founder corrections should change the wrong assumption only, not accidentally redesign the mission.**

Before proposing a correction, verify the relevant current canonical repository record, controlling governance and evidence. Do not presume disagreement means drift; a STOP may be justified by concrete security or authority risk.

## Five-part correction pattern

### A. State what Mission Control got right

Acknowledge valid disposition elements, locked stages and legitimate holds. This prevents unnecessary reopening.

> MC-268 correctly established that the Blueprint, EIS and Stage 13 package are locked and implementation is withheld.

### B. Identify the exact incorrect assumption

Quote or pinpoint the erroneous premise. Do not criticize or reject the entire disposition if only one line is wrong.

> The sequencing after Stage 13 is incorrect because the canonical Phase 1 operating pattern requires documentary suspension and a partial OLE handoff before moving to the next mission.

### C. Provide controlling authority or evidence

Use a precise approved source, durable mission record or Founder factual correction, with a verifiable reference.

> Apply `mission-control/SB-P-Phase 1 operating pattern.md`, relevant controlling sections.

Or:

> Founder factual correction: these identities are Founder-controlled test data, not external customer production data.

Distinguish verified repository facts from Founder-supplied factual context and uncertain inferences. A factual correction does not authorize exposing actual external customer records or live credentials.

### D. State what must change and what must remain unchanged

Be narrow and explicit:

> Reclassify SEC-262-01 only. Preserve all valid Stage 10 engineering findings. Do not reopen Stage 8. Do not regenerate the EIS. Do not create a new privacy mission.

Preserve valid implementation holds, already accepted artifacts, settled Founder decisions and unrelated risk findings.

### E. State the expected next lifecycle action

Give the **exact resume point** and any condition that still legitimately blocks continuation.

> Remove the incorrect security hold and continue Stage 10 → Stage 11 **if no independent blocker remains**.

Or:

> Record Implementation Authorization WITHHELD, create partial suspension/OLE artifacts, then proceed to the next approved mission after the applicable human-merge and post-merge verification boundary.

## Before issuing a correction

Confirm:

1. What part of the Mission Control decision is demonstrably wrong?
2. What controlling canonical evidence or explicit Founder fact establishes the correction?
3. Which accepted findings and holds must remain untouched?
4. What concrete harm does the disputed gate prevent? A gate protecting financial integrity, tenant isolation, authority, credentials, privacy or irreversible changes is generally material.
5. What is the **single narrowest safe correction** and **first unfinished lifecycle gate**?

If current GitHub evidence is unavailable, report the verification gap rather than claiming canonical certainty. Do not turn a factual correction into broader Product Truth, architecture, privacy, security or mission redesign without separately governed authority.

**The goal is not to win a disagreement with Mission Control. It is to restore truthful, safe forward movement.**
